# TermiSoc server administration — Rich Jeffery and 2004 infrastructure evidence

## Source

LinuxQuestions thread: *adduser and passwd in Fedora 1.92 is producing errors*, posted 18 May 2004 by `Rich@Termisoc` and subsequently updated by the same account.

URL: https://www.linuxquestions.org/questions/linux-general-1/adduser-and-passwrd-in-fedora-1-92-is-producing-errors-182976/

## Direct evidence

- The author describes trying to add a new user to the `Termsioc (University Of Plymouth CS) servers`.
- The account is displayed as `Rich@Termisoc`.
- The post signature says `Rich Jeffery` and `Termisoc President`.
- The technical problem concerned `adduser`, `useradd`, `passwd`, `/etc/passwd` and `/etc/shadow` behaviour on a TermiSoc server.
- The author later reports that the problem was resolved by removing a Linux security package.
- The thread therefore directly links a named TermiSoc president to active administration of the society's Unix/Linux infrastructure in May 2004.

## Corroboration

Slideserve preserves a presentation titled *The Future of Video Gaming* whose first slide identifies `Rich Jeffery President of TermiSoc`. The current Slideserve publication date is later than the historical context and should not be treated as the presentation date without further evidence.

URL: https://www.slideserve.com/quincy/the-future-of-video-gaming-powerpoint-ppt-presentation

## Interpretation

The LinuxQuestions record is stronger than a modern CV statement for infrastructure archaeology because it is a contemporaneous operational discussion and contains the author's explicit TermiSoc identity and role. It supports the conclusion that TermiSoc operated user-account-based Unix/Linux servers and that its president was directly involved in their administration at least in May 2004.

This does **not** independently establish the total number of servers, their physical location, or whether Rich Jeffery was the sole administrator.

## Confidence

- Rich Jeffery was TermiSoc President in May 2004: **High**.
- Rich Jeffery actively administered or maintained TermiSoc server infrastructure in May 2004: **High**.
- TermiSoc operated multiple Unix/Linux user-account servers in 2004: **High** for at least the existence of server(s) with user-account administration; **Medium** for plurality beyond the wording `servers` in the post.
- Physical server location: **Unverified**.
