# TermiSoc archaeology search matrix

## Purpose

Provide a repeatable search programme for reconstructing TermiSoc from surviving public evidence. Searches should distinguish direct contemporary evidence from later self-reported recollection and should be recorded in the relevant research directory before publication.

## Priority tracks

| Priority | Track | Targets | Current status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Historical web space | `termisoc.org/~*`, filenames, directories, image assets | Active; partial namespace recovered |
| 2 | Mailing lists | `@termisoc.org`, role signatures, `lists.termisoc.org` | Active; strong early evidence |
| 3 | Infrastructure | `area51`, `lug`, `lists`, `phpmyadmin`, `mooseblaster` | Active; strong evidence |
| 4 | People | members, officers, committee succession | Active; rapidly expanding |
| 5 | University/Union | Plymouth/UPSU society records, handbooks, newspapers | Active; public search so far sparse |
| 6 | Events | hack weekends, talks, LANs, installfests, AGMs | Active; 2011 hack weekend and other leads found |
| 7 | Linux/open source | Linux Format, Debian, FreeBSD, Perl, LUG archives | Active; strong 1999–2001 evidence |
| 8 | Images | personal galleries, archived image URLs, filename searches | Not yet systematic |
| 9 | Domain/DNS | WHOIS, DNS, nameserver and hosting chronology | Not yet systematic |
| 10 | Academic web studies | historical web-crawl datasets and link-analysis studies | Lead identified; no TermiSoc-specific record yet |

## Search procedure

1. Search exact domain and subdomain strings.
2. Search exact historical URLs and distinctive filenames discovered in results.
3. Search role titles and email addresses without requiring the TermiSoc name.
4. Search identified people against their own historical websites and archives.
5. Search University/UPSU material without `TermiSoc` to catch official naming variants.
6. Search events and artefacts independently of the society name.
7. Record both positive evidence and useful negative searches.
8. Save the research record before reporting completion.
9. Promote evidence to `source/` only after provenance and confidence are appropriate.
