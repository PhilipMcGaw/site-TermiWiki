# University of Plymouth CompSoc, RoboSoc, and Geek Society social-media evidence

Searched public web-indexed results for University of Plymouth references on LinkedIn and Facebook using CompSoc, Computer Society, RobSoc, Robotics Society, Geek Society, and Geek Club. Retrieval date: 2026-09-17.

## Findings

- [UoP Computer Society on LinkedIn](https://uk.linkedin.com/company/uop-computer-society) describes itself as the Official University of Plymouth Computer Society and links to the UPSU CompSoc page. This is current-society evidence, not evidence of historical TermiSoc. Confidence: High.
- [UPSU Robotics Society](https://www.upsu.com/societies/robotics/) identifies a current University of Plymouth Robotics Society, including Combat Bots, socials, and peer learning. Confidence: High.
- [Benjamin Job](https://fr.linkedin.com/in/benjaminjob) records "RoboSoc (Robotics society)" among University of Plymouth activities for 2018–2022. This is a retrospective alumni record. Confidence: Medium.
- [Christopher Jenkins](https://www.linkedin.com/in/chrisjenx) records "Termisoc (Computing Society)" and "Robotics Society" among University of Plymouth activities during 2007–2011, supporting co-existence of those named societies in one student account. Confidence: Medium.
- [Raven White](https://uk.linkedin.com/in/raven-white-5aa05384) records "Robotics Society" at University of Plymouth for 2011–2018. Confidence: Medium.
- Multiple LinkedIn alumni profiles name "Geek Society" among University of Plymouth activities, including [Charlotte Wotton](https://uk.linkedin.com/in/charlotte-wotton-9a8868118), [James Roberts](https://uk.linkedin.com/in/jamesroberts12), [Matthew Copperwaite](https://uk.linkedin.com/in/mattcopp), and [Joseph Kellaway](https://uk.linkedin.com/in/joseph-kellaway-604a22128). Collectively these support the existence of a Geek Society, but not its exact relationship to CompSoc or TermiSoc. Confidence: Medium.
- [James Woodland](https://uk.linkedin.com/in/james-woodland) refers to a "geek society esports section" while describing Plymouth University Esports Society activity in 2019–2020. The organisational relationship is unclear. Confidence: Low–Medium.
- A 2018 [UPSU fresher's booklet](https://www.readkong.com/page/medsoc-2018-fresher-s-booklet-7259962) lists "Geek Society" among University of Plymouth societies. The copy is hosted by a third-party mirror. Confidence: Medium–High for the listing; Medium for provenance.
- A 2018 student account describes Geek Society video-game socials and film screenings: [GIGA blog](https://gigakitianamcewan.wordpress.com/2018/11/27/researching-uni-options/). Confidence: Low–Medium.

## Facebook search result

Public indexed searches did not surface a clearly attributable Facebook page or group for University of Plymouth CompSoc, RoboSoc, or Geek Society. This does not establish that none existed: pages may be unindexed, renamed, private, deleted, or login-visible only.

## Interpretation

The evidence supports three layers: a current official UoP Computer Society/CompSoc presence; a current UPSU Robotics Society with RoboSoc appearing in an alumni record; and a historically attested Geek Society in alumni profiles and a 2018 fresher's booklet. The evidence does not establish that Geek Society was the same organisation as CompSoc or TermiSoc. Christopher Jenkins's record instead directly distinguishes Termisoc (Computing Society) from Robotics Society.
