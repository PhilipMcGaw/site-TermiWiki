# TermiSoc people stub expansion

## Purpose

The repository contains a large set of legacy TermiPeople pages that are still stubs. This record starts a focused expansion queue, keeping identity research separate from published historical claims.

## Initial inventory

The most useful first targets are people who have distinctive aliases, committee roles, or links to the early TermiSoc history. The current inventory includes stubs for Christine Harrison, Matt Groves, Marcus Adams, Paul Fletcher, James Hickman, Andrew Burridge, Jeff Powell, and many others. Each person should be researched separately and only promoted when the identity and historical relationship are adequately supported.

## First search pass

### Paul Fletcher — promising lead, not yet identified

A public University of Plymouth news page identifies **Paul Fletcher FBCS** as receiving an honorary fellowship in 2022 for contributions to computing and public-service cyber security. An independent executive biography identifies a Paul Fletcher associated with Nominet and states that he received a University of Plymouth honorary fellowship for services to computing.[^1][^2]

This is a promising lead because the legacy TermiSoc stub is **Paul “Fletch” Fletcher**, but the available results do not yet prove that the modern computing professional is the same individual as the historical TermiSoc member. Do not merge the identities without a matching course date, age, historical role, or other corroboration.

### Marcus Adams — possible Plymouth identity

A public LinkedIn search result identifies a Marcus Adams at Wipro and includes the University of Plymouth. This is only a possible identity match for the legacy **Marcus “Samwise” Adams** page; the result does not yet expose TermiSoc activity or sufficient biographical detail.[^3]

### Christine Harrison — unresolved

Search results for the exact name were dominated by unrelated company and public-record entries. No reliable TermiSoc or University of Plymouth match was identified in this pass.

### Matt Groves — unresolved

Search results were dominated by an English footballer named Matt Groves and generic LinkedIn directory results. No reliable TermiSoc or University of Plymouth match was identified in this pass.

## Research method

For each stub:

1. Extract the legacy alias, roles, dates, and links from the page and nearby historical pages.
2. Search exact name plus alias, `TermiSoc`, Plymouth, University of Plymouth, and distinctive technical or social terms.
3. Separate identity evidence from evidence of TermiSoc participation.
4. Record negative or ambiguous searches here or in a person-specific research record.
5. Update `source/TermiPeople/` only when the evidence supports a cautious, sourced expansion.
