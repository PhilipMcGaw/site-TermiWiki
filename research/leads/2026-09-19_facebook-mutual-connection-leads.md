# TermiSoc Facebook mutual-connection leads

## Scope and handling

On 19 September 2026, the project maintainer supplied a set of Facebook “members with things in common” results from the private TermiSoc group. The results show mutual-connection overlap with people already represented in the archive.

This is a **prioritisation queue**, not a membership list or historical roster. A mutual connection is not evidence that a person was a TermiSoc member, held office, attended a particular event, or should be contacted. Current employment, education, location, and follower-count text displayed by Facebook are intentionally omitted because they are neither necessary nor suitable evidence for historical claims.

**Sequence:** revisit these only after the existing people pages and their direct documentary evidence have been improved as far as practical. First check the archived web material, already-preserved group files, committee graphics, and existing outreach replies; use a public profile only to corroborate a candidate, never to infer a historical role.
