## `~j` spreadsheet retrieval check

On 17 September 2026, the live URLs for `example.xls` and `SWdata.xls` each returned HTTP 404. A public Internet Archive CDX lookup for the two exact URLs returned no preserved successful spreadsheet capture. No file was therefore downloaded or added to the vault. The 10 November 2003 PC Review thread remains the direct surviving evidence: it describes `example.xls` as approximately 180 KB and `SWdata.xls` as the approximately 2 MB original database.

Arquivo.pt's public index returned no capture for the `example.xls` URL. Common Crawl's publicly listed collections begin in 2008, several years after the 2003 forum post that links the files, so it is a low-probability recovery source rather than proof that the files were never crawled elsewhere. The UK Web Archive and University of Plymouth/UPSU or former-member backups remain plausible avenues, but were not queried as an accessible public binary archive in this pass.
