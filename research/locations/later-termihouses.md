# Later TermiHouse groups

## Access and handling

The project maintainer supplied private Facebook Messenger and private-group screenshots on 19 September 2026. They contain correspondence and member information, so they are **not copied into this repository**. The project maintainer retains the original image collection.

This note is a privacy-minimised index of the historical claims that need later corroboration. It is not evidence for publication in `source/`.

## Direct evidence from the supplied material

- Current screenshots show private Facebook groups titled **TermiHau5** and **TermiHau6**. The screenshots show five and three members respectively at capture time.
- The screenshots establish that these group names exist; they do not establish the dates of the houses, their addresses, formal tenancies, or a complete historical occupant list.

## Later recollection

A later private recollection by Matt Farrell describes a sequence of numbered TermiHouse groups after TermiHouse 2:

- **TermiHouse 4** is recalled as having involved several former members, including Jacob Gathercole, but the wording and full membership list are not sufficiently clear to transcribe as a reliable roster.
- **TermiHau5** is remembered as a later group.
- **TermiHau6** is recalled as having included Robbie Rowe and other people not fully identified in the material.
- A seventh house is uncertain; an eighth was said not to have occurred.

These are later recollections rather than contemporary documentary evidence. They should not be used to assign membership, addresses or dates without corroboration.

## Relationship to established records

- [[termihouse-1|TermiHouse 1]] is documented in the historic wiki corpus as the 2005–06 house.
- [[../../source/Glossary/termihouse2|TermiHouse 2]] is documented at 5 Sea View Avenue in 2007, with a separately preserved dated family-meal photograph.
- The supplied material makes the previous claim that no evidence existed for later numbered houses obsolete as a research status, but it does not yet provide publication-grade evidence for a complete sequence.

## Corroboration priorities

1. Obtain a dated contemporaneous post, photograph, tenancy document or event record that names any later TermiHouse.
2. Ask former residents for permission to record their own association, dates and any publishable photographs.
3. Establish whether “TermiHau” was a later spelling used for the same continuing house tradition.
4. Confirm whether TermiHouse 3 existed, and resolve the numbering and chronology of Houses 4–6.

## Publication impact

Do not amend public TermiHouse pages yet. A future public page should state only corroborated chronology and consented associations.
