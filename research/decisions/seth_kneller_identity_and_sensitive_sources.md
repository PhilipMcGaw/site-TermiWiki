# Seth Kneller: identity matching and sensitive modern sources

## Decision

The project owner has confirmed that the person in the 2023 BBC report is the same individual as the historical TermiSoc member. This is recorded as project-owner confirmation, rather than as an independently documented identity match.

The BBC report, its allegations, and related modern personal information MUST still be kept separate from the published historical profile unless a specific, privacy-reviewed editorial purpose is established. The report is not evidence for historical TermiSoc activity.

## Rationale

The BBC article concerns a recent criminal case and is sensitive personal information. The historical TermiSoc material is substantially older and does not, by itself, establish the identity match; the match is supplied by the project owner. Repeating or linking the modern report from the archive could still expose sensitive personal information without a clear archival purpose.

The existing [[source/TermiPeople/seth_kneller|Seth Kneller]] page contains personal and biographical details from legacy material. Any future editing of that page should follow data-minimisation principles, preserve only historically relevant information, and avoid adding modern identifying details unless there is a clear archival purpose and reliable consent or public-interest basis.
