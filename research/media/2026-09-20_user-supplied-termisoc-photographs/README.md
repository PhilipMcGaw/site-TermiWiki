# User-supplied 2005 photographs

This directory preserves 19 original JPEG photographs supplied by the project maintainer on 20 September 2026. The files remain research-only: several show identifiable people, and no publication permission, captions, or name identifications are recorded for them.

The embedded camera metadata reports a Nikon D70 and creation times between 22 September and 25 November 2005. Metadata can be changed, but the contiguous 29 September sequence, its visible fair material, and the dates provide strong internal support for the described event context.

The 29 September photographs show TermiSoc's stand at the Sports & Societies Fair in the Main Hall, table 42. Their visible printed promotion advertises “500MB of webspace, spamproof e-mail and your own web address for £5?” and identifies this as a student discount. See the [accompanying evidence record](../../evidence/2026-09-20_termisoc-2005-photograph-batch.md).
