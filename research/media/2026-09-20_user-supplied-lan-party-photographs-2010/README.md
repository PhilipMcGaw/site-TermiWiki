# User-supplied LAN-party photographs, 2010

This directory preserves 58 original photographs supplied by the project maintainer on 20 September 2026 and described by them as photographs from a LAN party. The set shows an indoor gathering with networked computers, laptops, peripherals, headphones, and identifiable attendees.

The maintainer has authorised publication of this supplied material. It remains research media: do not embed it in `source/` or generated output, identify people from appearance, or infer names, roles, venue, or organiser without independent documentary support. See the [accompanying evidence record](../../evidence/2026-09-20_user-supplied-lan-party-photographs-2010.md).

## Embedded timestamps

Fifty-six files carry camera creation times on 30 January 2010, from 13:25:24 to 21:12:17. `DSC_3608 2.jpeg` and `DSC_3619 2.jpeg` instead carry times on 9 July 2010. The maintainer supplied all 58 together as one LAN-party set, but the timestamp difference must be retained until its cause or event relationship is independently confirmed.

The originals have dimensions of either 3,008 × 2,000 px, 3,024 × 1,998 px, or 2,000 × 3,008 px. The directory occupies approximately 67 MB.
