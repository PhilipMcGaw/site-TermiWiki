# User-supplied Oxford steampunk-event photographs, 2010

This directory preserves four original photographs supplied by the project maintainer on 20 September 2026 and described by them as photographs from a steampunk event in Oxford. The set contains identifiable people and is retained for research only. Do not embed it in `source/` or generated output, or identify people from appearance, without explicit confirmation and appropriate consent.

The images retain camera creation times on 16 January 2010, from 12:25:46 to 14:05:49. One photograph includes a sign for “Major Tinker's Emporium”, describing itself as “The Very Finest Notions & Trinkets For the Discerning Steampunk” and showing `http://tinkers-emporium.synthasite.com`.
