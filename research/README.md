# TermiWiki research corpus

This directory contains the shared research and evidence base used to reconstruct TermiSoc history and related computing-society history.

It is deliberately separate from the Obsidian vault in `source/`. Research can therefore be collected, challenged, corroborated, and revised without treating every discovery as published historical fact.

## How to use it

Start with `research/AGENTS.md` and the relevant subdirectory instructions. Record useful evidence as soon as it is found, including its provenance and what it actually establishes.

A good research record answers:

1. What was investigated?
2. What source was found?
3. What does the source directly establish?
4. What is inferred rather than directly stated?
5. What corroborates or contradicts it?
6. What remains unresolved?
7. Does an existing page in `source/` need review?

The research corpus is intended to be usable by humans and by different LLM agents. Do not rely on any one model's conversation history as the project's memory.

Open preservation and verification actions are tracked in [research/TODO.md](TODO.md) using the `TODO` and `ROADMAP` markers recognised by the workspace's Better TODO Tree extension.

## Evidence hierarchy

Use source criticism rather than a numerical scoring system. Contemporary primary evidence is generally more direct than later recollection, while secondary directories and search results can still be useful leads. Record the nature and limitations of each source.

## Publication

Material is not automatically published merely because it appears here. Promotion into `source/` requires appropriate evidence and provenance. Unresolved material can remain in `research/leads/` until corroborated.
