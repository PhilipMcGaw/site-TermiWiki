# Skippy and the Power Racing Series discussion

## Direct evidence

- A message posted under the name **Skippy** on 4 July 2012 proposed exploring a UK version of the Power Racing Series, referring to a Wired article about modified toy vehicles and asking for opinions on trying the idea.
- The discussion was visible in the Swindon Makerspace Google Group and was cross-posted to `TermiSoc@termisoc.org`, connecting the later makerspace discussion with the TermiSoc mailing-list address.
- A reply in the thread says, “That does look like fun,” and quotes the original message from Skippy.[^thread]

## Interpretation and limits

The page directly establishes use of the Skippy alias in a 2012 technical and maker-community discussion. It does not state the writer’s legal name. The project owner identifies this Skippy with Philip McGaw, but that identification remains a separately recorded project-owner statement rather than something proved by this page alone.[^identity]

This is later evidence about continuing online activity and cross-community connections, not evidence that the Power Racing Series project was built or run by TermiSoc.
