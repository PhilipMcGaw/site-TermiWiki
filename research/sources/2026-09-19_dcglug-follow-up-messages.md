# DCGLUG archive follow-up messages 00076, 00079, and 00248

## Source and retrieval

All three messages were retrieved from the public Devon & Cornwall Linux Users' Group archive on **19 September 2026**:

- [Message 00076 — c programming](https://www.dcglug.org.uk/archive-Nov00-May01/msg00076.html)
- [Message 00079 — reply](https://www.dcglug.org.uk/archive-Nov00-May01/msg00079.html)
- [Message 00248 — VMware pack](https://www.dcglug.org.uk/archive-Nov00-May01/msg00248.html)

## Direct evidence

### Message 00076

Paul Sutton's message, dated **25 November 2000**, asks how to clear the screen in a C programme and requests recommendations for C programming mailing lists. It is addressed through `lug-list at area51.termisoc.org` and includes the TermiSoc-hosted unsubscribe address `lists.termisoc.org`.

### Message 00079

Neil Stone's reply, dated **27 November 2000**, suggests an ANSI escape sequence for clearing the screen. It is a direct follow-up to message 00076 and uses the same `area51.termisoc.org` reply and sender infrastructure.

### Message 00248

Alex Charrett's message, dated **20 February 2001**, reports receiving a VMware promotional pack for the Linux Users' Group. He lists the contents, says VMware may provide a full licence for user-group and demonstration purposes, and considers requesting evaluation licences for a future event. The message is addressed to `lug-list at termisoc.org` and replies through `area51.termisoc.org`.

## Interpretation

Messages 00076 and 00079 provide contemporary evidence of technical discussion and functioning TermiSoc-hosted DCGLUG infrastructure in November 2000. Message 00248 directly connects Alex Charrett with the LUG's technology outreach and potential demonstration activity in February 2001. None of the messages by itself establishes a TermiSoc officer role or proves who operated each server.
