# TermiSoc public X/Twitter profile and timeline

## Scope

This record preserves a signed-out, public inspection of the historical TermiSoc account at <https://x.com/TermiSoc> on 19 September 2026. It is a provenance record and a small, directly observed timeline sample; it is **not** a complete export of the account.

- name: **TermiSoc**;
- handle: **@TermiSoc**;
- description: “The Plymouth University Computing and Technology Society.”;

The profile's contemporaneous society description and `termisoc.org` link make this a high-confidence official TermiSoc account.

One reviewed photo post is already historically substantive. On **19 January 2014**, TermiSoc addressed Exeter Computer Soc (`@excompsci`) asking whether there was “an award for the most over-abundance of tech?”, accompanied by an image of a table of laptops, electronics and cabling. Exeter Computer Soc replied that TermiSoc would “definitely win by a mile” and referred to “Full steam ahead for Plymouth.” This is direct contemporary evidence of a friendly inter-society exchange and a technology-heavy TermiSoc activity, though the image alone does not establish its event name or venue. The original post is <https://x.com/TermiSoc/status/424857427440570368>; the reply is <https://x.com/excompsci/status/424897626816118784>. The reply’s attached X-delivered image is preserved in [[../media/2026-09-19_termisoc-x-reply-media/README]].

The oldest rendered photo-post URL currently resolves to **17 November 2012**, with the text “And so it begins.” and X's Plymouth location label: <https://x.com/TermiSoc/status/269748945352273920>. The image did not render reliably during review, so this establishes an early dated-photo lead only; it does not establish the depicted activity.

## Access limits

The public profile and its initial posts were readable without signing in. The authenticated follow-up made the Photos view available, but X did not expose its original image-file assets through the browser's read-only asset inventory. The displayed count should not be treated as proof that all 290 posts remain individually accessible.

## Archival approach

- Preserve the public profile URL and individual status URLs as the canonical public references.
- Add dated event, committee, infrastructure or society-transition posts to separate evidence records as they are individually reviewed.
- Do not bulk-copy the complete timeline into `source/`; only publish derived historical claims after relevance and corroboration review.
