# TermiSoc T-shirt and hoodie order, October 2015

## Source and retrieval

The project maintainer supplied a transcription of a 7 October 2015 post by Eleanor Kate Milner in the private TermiSoc Facebook group on 17 September 2026. An authenticated review of the supplied group-member route identifies the same profile as a former TermiSoc member, providing a high-confidence bridge to the existing Eleanor Milner research entry.

The transcription contained a student email address. It is intentionally not copied into this research record.

## Direct evidence

- The post announces an order for TermiSoc T-shirts and hoodies.
- Estimated prices were £12 for a T-shirt and £21 for a hoodie.
- Orders were open from 7 October 2015 and needed to be placed before 26 October, when the order and payment collection were planned.
- The post states that the prices could change depending on order volume, with notice promised in advance.

## Interpretation

This is direct evidence that TermiSoc was arranging branded clothing in October 2015, and it supplies a date and price context for the existing shirt photographs. It does not identify the final order quantities, final prices, garment design, supplier, or whether the order was completed.

## Publication boundary

The source is a private Facebook-group post. This record stays in `research/`; the original post text and personal contact detail must not be reproduced in the published vault without deliberate approval and redaction.
