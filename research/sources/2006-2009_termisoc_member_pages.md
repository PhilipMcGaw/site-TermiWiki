# Archived TermiSoc WikiHistory member pages, 2006–2009

## What was investigated

Internet Archive snapshots supplied for the WikiHistory current-members category, individual member profiles, and two external sites linked from a member profile.

## Direct evidence

- The 31 December 2006 `Current Members` category lists 14 articles: Andrew Gill, Ben A'Lee, Carl Notman, Chris Morris, Damien Hopton, Dan Cosser, Edward McCaughan, Gemma Peter, Lenny Sim, Mark Hingston, Philip McGaw, Rich Jeffery, Seth Kneller, and Tim Huntington.[^current-members]
- Damien Hopton's 12 April 2006 profile records him as a member in 2005–2006 and LAN Game Organiser in 2006. A later snapshot records him as Social Secretary for 2006, so the role chronology needs preserving as recorded rather than silently resolved.[^damien-2006][^damien-2007]
- Edward McCaughan's July and September 2007 profiles record Projects Officer for 2006–2007 and Assistant Technical Officer in 2007, alongside the aliases “MadScientist” and “sertorius”.[^edward-2007][^edward-2007-later]
- Rich Jeffery's September and December 2006 profiles record Safety Officer for 2003–2004 and President from 2004 onwards. The profile describes his involvement in the 2004 recruitment effort and the simultaneous hard-drive failure that reduced the group.[^rich-2006][^rich-2006-later]
- Tim Huntington's June 2008 profile says he undertook a geology degree from 2004 onwards, initially Applied Geology and later MGeol, and was interested in Delphi and geoscience subjects. It categorises him as a current member.[^tim-2008]
- Ben A'Lee's September and December 2006 profiles record Secretary for 2004–2005 and Head Technical Officer for 2005–2006, followed by both Secretary and Technical Officer from 2006 onwards. The page is written as a humorous retrospective profile and should not be treated as an independent constitutional record.[^ben-2006][^ben-2006-later]
- The September 2007 Plymouth CATHSOC homepage identifies Damien Hopton as Chairman and describes the society as open to everyone, with weekly Mass, social events, trips, and faith-related courses.[^cathsoc]
- The August 2007 Abacus Projects homepage is a Joomla site with no useful evidence in the captured text beyond the project name and site structure. Its relationship to Hopton is not established by this snapshot alone.[^abacus]

## Unresolved or limited retrievals

The supplied snapshots for Andrew Gill, Chris Morris, Philip McGaw, Seth Kneller, and Dan Cosser returned an Internet Archive compression error during retrieval. The later Ben A'Lee, Damien Hopton, Edward McCaughan, and Rich Jeffery snapshots supplied for comparison were partly redundant; the accessible versions are recorded above. The missing pages remain leads for later recovery or corroboration.

The member profiles are WikiHistory pages and often contain humour, self-description, or retrospective recollection. Dated roles should be checked against AGM minutes, constitutions, or contemporary notices before being promoted as definitive office-holder records.
