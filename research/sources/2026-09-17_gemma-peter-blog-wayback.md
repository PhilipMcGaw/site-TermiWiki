# Gemma Peter blog archive

## Source and retrieval

Wayback captures of `http://blog.gemmapeter.co.uk/` were reviewed on 17 September 2026. The archive has 46 captures between 17 February 2006 and 16 August 2022. A 2006 capture preserves blog material dating back to November 2004.

## Direct evidence

- A post dated 9 January 2005, filed under both “Termisoc” and “University”, says Gemma had missed the “Termisoc guys” over Christmas. This directly supports her contemporaneous social connection to the society.
- A post dated 4 February 2006 says she was preparing a TermiSoc Blender tutorial and links to `termisoc.org`.
- The 2006 blog navigation contains a “Termisoc” category with one post, confirming that the author categorised the January 2005 entry as TermiSoc-related.

## Interpretation

The personal blog is a primary contemporary source for Gemma’s own connection to the society and her planned tutorial. It does not, by itself, verify all historical offices listed on the migrated people page; those require their own sources.

The blogroll lists sites associated with Ben, Dan, Rich, Ruth, Ryan, Seth, Skippy and others. This is useful for discovery, but a blogroll link alone is not proof of TermiSoc membership or a particular identity.
