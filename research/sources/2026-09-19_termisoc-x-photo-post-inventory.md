## Preservation status

On 19 September 2026, all twenty photo-post image renditions were successfully retrieved through authenticated X post pages and saved to [[../media/2026-09-19_termisoc-x-photo-posts/README]]. The account's public 400×400 profile PNG is retained in the same collection but is separately labelled. The post collection contains X-delivered medium-resolution JPEG renditions with per-file SHA-256 hashes; it does not claim to preserve full-resolution originals.

The project maintainer subsequently authorised public use of these X-delivered renditions on the wiki. The research originals remain in this collection; separately copied public renditions are embedded in [[../../source/termisoc_on_twitter]] from `source/Media/x_termisoc/`.

## Handling

- Treat each status page and its embedded image as public, third-party-hosted material until separately captured with provenance.
- Review each post's date, caption, depicted people and rights implications before copying an image to `source/Media/`.
- Do not embed this research-media collection in `source/`.
