# Case's Early History of TermiSoc — 2001 capture

## Source and retrieval

Internet Archive capture dated 24 December 2001, reviewed in a browser on 17 September 2026:

<https://web.archive.org/web/20011224120321/http://www.termisoc.org/termisoc/history1.php>

## Direct evidence

- The page is titled *Case's Early History of TermiSoc* and describes TermiSoc as also known as UPSUCS.
- It presents a retrospective account of 1991–1994, including the Group of Three, Cadmus, Cheeseplants House, Hades, Freshers Faires, Area51, and Flux BBS.
- It gives contemporary-to-publication spellings and nicknames: James “Simkin” Hickman, Marcus “Samwise” Adams, Paul “Fletch” Fletcher, Robert “Grimond” Dymond, Jon “Arashi” Care, Andrew “Acheron/Case” Burridge, Paul “Nemo” Bland, Alex “Veeg” McLean, Alex “BTF” Allmont, and Ashley “Arp” Pomeroy.
- For the 1992 executive, it describes Jon Care as **Vice President**. The later curated compilation currently labels that role President.
- The page includes links to a second history page, an executive-history page, a kit list, a constitution, member homepages, and a 2001 Christmas Day event.

## Interpretation

This is an important early published form of the account later represented in [[../../source/the_abridged_history|The Abridged History]]. It is stronger provenance for the wording and roles it states than later transcription alone, but remains retrospective testimony by Case rather than contemporary evidence from 1991–1994.

The Jon Care role discrepancy should remain unresolved until the 2001 and later versions are compared alongside executive lists or other contemporary records. The 2001 page MUST NOT be used to silently rewrite the curated history without that comparison.
