# Targeted Facebook group search

## Source and access

User-authorised browser access to the private `TermiSoc` Facebook group, searched 17 September 2026. The group describes itself as “The University of Plymouth Terminal Society”.

## Direct evidence

- Search for `TermiHouse` returned a dated photograph. Its visible 26 August 2007 caption states, “The first ‘family’ meal in the TermiHouse!”. The recovered image and full provenance are retained separately in [TermiHouse evidence](../evidence/2026-09-17_facebook-termihouse/README.md). It establishes use of a place called TermiHouse, but not its address.
- Searches for `FOOCHRE` and `area51.termisoc.org` returned no results.
- The group’s **Files** tab currently exposes three historical uploads: `Termi Coding 1.zip` (archive, 26 October 2015), `LAN Information.docx` (18 March 2015), and `PATW - 2013.pdf` (20 February 2013). The first two are directly TermiSoc-relevant candidates for review and preservation; the third needs a relevance check before collection.
- The **Events** tab exposes a dated `Christmas 24hour LAN & Gaming Party` on 17 December 2016. Other immediately listed events were unrelated social-event shares and were not collected.
- The related private **TermiCraft: The TermiSoc Minecraft Server** group has 19 members and showed no past or upcoming events in its Events tab at retrieval. Its event archive therefore yielded no additional event-page candidates in this pass.
- The **Media** tab exposes older images, but the first inspected examples were uncaptioned personal/social photographs. They were not downloaded. The already-recorded TermiHouse 2 photograph remains the clear historical image candidate from the first pass.
- A targeted search for `heritage` located a 9 September 2017 post containing six historic UPSU membership cards. The image is retained as research-only evidence because it includes a member's name, signature, and membership-number details; see [heritage-card evidence](../evidence/2026-09-17_facebook-termisoc-heritage-cards/README.md).
- A targeted search for `carrot` located Deric Toms's 13 August 2017 post of *Eat More Carrots!*, described as an A5 leaflet from circa 1994. A matching local leaflet image already existed in `source/media/`; it is now documented in [[../../source/eat_more_carrots_leaflet|a curated source page]].
- No CAPTCHA, access challenge, or platform warning occurred. No messages, reactions, comments, invitations, profile edits, or posts were made.

## Interpretation

The TermiSoc group is a viable source for manual historical review. The two directly named 2015 files and the 2016 LAN event are stronger follow-up targets than browsing uncaptioned media. TermiCraft remains useful for Minecraft-specific posts, media, and files, but does not currently supply event records. The initial targeted terms produced no new text evidence for the FOOCHRE or server-host bridge.

## Outstanding work

- Open and assess the `Termi Coding 1.zip` and `LAN Information.docx` attachments. Preserve only material with useful TermiSoc historical content, retaining original filenames and provenance.
- Search for `Linux`, `LAN`, `Sun Lab`, `Plymouth Computing`, `termisoc.org`, and named committee members, recording only material that independently supports a historical claim.
- Inspect the 2016 LAN event for venue, organiser, description, and attached media; treat it as group-private until a publication decision is made.

None from this pass; the result is an access and lead assessment only.
