# TermiSoc external references: media, DCLUG, and later profiles

## Direct evidence

- The Wikimedia Commons file description for `Guru meditation.gif` records the image as a two-frame, 2,548-byte GIF measuring 652 × 93 pixels. It identifies the source as `mooseblaster.termisoc.org/404.htm`, the author as “Mooseblaster”, and the licence as public domain.[^guru]
- A DCLUG mailing-list message dated 21 September 2005 is from Benjamin A'Lee and signs off “Termisoc Tech Officer”. The message concerns installing Subversion on Red Hat 9 and provides direct evidence of A'Lee's technical-officer role at that date.[^dclug]
- Nick Charlton's public archive lists technical and project posts from 2008 onwards, including “Configuring an SSH banner on Debian”, “DNS Testing Tools”, “Introduction to SQLite 2 with PHP 5”, and “Jekyll and GitHub”. The archive page alone does not establish the author's TermiSoc membership or dates of involvement.[^nick-archives]
- The supplied SlideServe page is a 26-slide “Short PHP tutorial”, uploaded by `gotzon` and dated 25 March 2019. It is a later public presentation and does not, by itself, establish a historical TermiSoc tutorial or its authorship.[^php-tutorial]

## Modern profile leads

The supplied LinkedIn URLs for Nick Charlton, Jamie Pates, Jamie Woods, Jason Hill, Edward Knapp, Aaron Newman, Richard Gibson, Jack Hardaker, Joshua von Eicken, Chris, Christopher Pratt, Michael Wade, Aaron Trevena, and Milutin Manojlovic are retained as leads for identity and biographical corroboration. Modern profile information should be used sparingly, checked for current accuracy, and not treated as contemporary evidence of historical TermiSoc activity without an explicit dated association.

## Publication impact

The DCLUG message may support a dated role note on [[source/TermiPeople/ben_a_lee|Ben A'Lee]], subject to comparison with existing AGM and WikiHistory evidence. The Guru Meditation file may be relevant to a future media or 404-page entry, and it has now been preserved locally as `source/media/mooseblaster_guru_meditation.gif`.
