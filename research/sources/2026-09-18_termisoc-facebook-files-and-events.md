## Interpretation

The group retained a small but high-value set of downloadable society material from 2013 and 2015. The listed event feed is not, by itself, a society event register: it includes ordinary member shares alongside the Christmas LAN.

## Direct evidence: twentieth-anniversary statement

On **6 October 2011**, [Alex McLean](https://www.facebook.com/groups/2342018784/user/643941850/) posted in the TermiSoc group: “Just noticed that TermiSoc is 20 years old! Happy anniversary, and congratulations to all who have sailed in her.” ([Facebook post](https://www.facebook.com/groups/termisoc/posts/10150335456803785/)).

This is a dated retrospective statement consistent with the archive and LinkedIn-group claim that TermiSoc was founded in **1991**. It corroborates that year but is not contemporaneous evidence of the founding itself.
