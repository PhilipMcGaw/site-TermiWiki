## Interpretation

The Steam group directly establishes a dated TermiSoc presence on Steam by October 2009 and provides contemporary 2010 evidence of organised Team Fortress 2 and Urban Terror activity associated with the group. The announcements support the existence of planned gaming events and a game server, but do not by themselves establish attendance, completion, committee roles, or the real identities of Steam accounts.
