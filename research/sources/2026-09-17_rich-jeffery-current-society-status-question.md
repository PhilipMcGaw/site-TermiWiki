# Question about TermiSoc's current society status

## Source and retrieval

The project maintainer supplied the text of a post by Rich Jeffery in the private TermiSoc Facebook group on 17 September 2026, with the group-member URL. The supplied text asks whether anyone is in touch with “the current society”, says that `termisoc.org` is dead, and says that TermiSoc no longer appears in the UPSU A–Z societies list.

The authenticated group search confirms that Rich Jeffery is the relevant group member, but Facebook’s current search interface did not expose the original post date or a stable post permalink during retrieval.

## Direct evidence

- At the time the post was written, its author believed the TermiSoc domain was unavailable.
- The author also believed TermiSoc was absent from the UPSU society directory then visible to them.
- The wording is a question to other members, not an official announcement of closure, merger, or deregistration.

The project maintainer supplied associated replies on 17 September 2026:

- A member says that the society had “died and come back before”. This is a recollection, not a dated service-status record.
- Alex McLean says he no longer has a screenshot of his ASCII Flux login page, but says that the then-current system could be reached with `ssh bbs@fluxbbs.org`. The project maintainer records the Flux BBS web address as <https://www.fluxbbs.org/>.
- Alex Charrett identifies <https://www.fluxbbs.org/welcome.txt> as the oldest Flux welcome-banner version he could find.

The `welcome.txt` URL did not resolve during retrieval on 17 September 2026, so its contents and date remain unverified. Wayback has no captures for either the HTTP or HTTPS form of that exact path.

## Interpretation

This is useful evidence of uncertainty around TermiSoc’s contemporary status at an as-yet undated point. The accompanying recollection makes clear that a dead public domain is not a reliable indicator of permanent organisational closure: both the society and Flux had periods of interruption and recovery. The evidence is compatible with the later February 2017 merger announcement, but it cannot establish the date, cause, or formal status of a closure on its own. It must not be used to replace the dated merger evidence.

## Limits and publication decision

The evidence comes from a private group and is retained in `research/` only. No private post text, people data, or link is being added to the published `source/` vault without a deliberate publication decision.
