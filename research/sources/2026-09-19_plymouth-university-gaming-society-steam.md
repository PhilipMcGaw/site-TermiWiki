# Plymouth University Gaming Society Steam group

## Source and access

The public Steam group at <https://steamcommunity.com/groups/PlymUni> was reviewed on 19 September 2026 through the authenticated browser session. No membership request, post, comment, or other account change was made.

## Direct evidence

- Group title: **Plymouth University Gaming Society**; short name **PlymUni**.
- Steam displays **Founded: 1 September, 2015**, location **United Kingdom (Great Britain)**, language **English**, and **124 members** at retrieval.
- The group describes itself as the Steam group of the **Plymouth University eSports society**, saying that it played PC and console games and invited discussion and new members.
- The announcements listing displayed five posts:
  - **26 September 2015**, “Welcome to the society & remember to fill in the Active Members list!”, by **TABBY** (`tabichu`). It asks members to complete an active-members form, points to a Google spreadsheet showing other members' in-game names and games, and says to add people from the Steam group. [Announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/groups/PlymUni/announcements/detail/110693025689988610)
  - **7 October 2015**, “Sign up to get put in a team for NUEL LoL!”, by **TABBY** (`tabichu`). It describes the National University Esports League, League of Legends teams, the first NUEL date as 1 November, and a Best of the South West team. [Announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/groups/PlymUni/announcements/detail/92679739198630714)
  - **17 February 2016**, “SPRING LAN”, by **TUNDZ** (`tundz`). It advertises a £5 LAN entry including refreshments, infrastructure, and one tournament, with League of Legends, Counter-Strike, and Smash 4 tournaments; it also advertises PUGS-branded T-shirts and gives a society contact address. [Announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/groups/PlymUni/announcements/detail/988908276482884185)
  - **19 February 2016**, “SPRING LAN: UPDATE 1”, by **TUNDZ** (`tundz`). It says the LAN was the society's second, links a Facebook event and UPSU society page, gives a £5 ticket, arrival/setup arrangements, tournament timing, and names **Babatunde Benjamin** in the sign-off. [Announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/groups/PlymUni/announcements/detail/988908370233055544)
  - **6 May 2016**, “Survey”, by **TUNDZ** (`tundz`), asking members to complete a Qualtrics survey. [Announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/groups/PlymUni/announcements/detail/957390230039334664)

## Interpretation and boundary

This is direct evidence for a Plymouth University Gaming Society/eSports group active from September 2015 through at least May 2016, including LAN and university esports activity. It is not evidence that Plymouth University Gaming Society was legally or organisationally identical to historical TermiSoc. The Steam archive does show a later-group context relevant to the wider University of Plymouth computing-society landscape.

The announcement account names **TUNDZ**, **TABBY**, and the sign-off **Babatunde Benjamin** are retained as displayed. No identity bridge is asserted between these handles and named people without separate corroboration.

The public Steam profile [pixelchu](https://steamcommunity.com/id/pixelchu_x) displayed membership in both this group and the historical TermiSoc group during the same review. This is direct account-level overlap only; it does not establish organisational identity, shared governance, or formal partnership.
