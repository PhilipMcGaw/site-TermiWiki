## Direct evidence

The supplied LinkedIn group description identifies TermiSoc as the Plymouth University Computing and Technology Society, formerly the Computing Society. It claims that the society was established in 1991 and offered members a range of facilities.

These statements are later organisational self-description. They do not by themselves prove the exact 1991 foundation date or establish a formal renaming date, but they corroborate the use of Computing Society and Computing and Technology Society as descriptions of TermiSoc and point to a services page as a further research target.

The Mattsi Jansky and Danbjorn URLs are retained as identity and membership-profile leads connected to existing TermiSoc people records. No additional biographical claims are made from the inaccessible LinkedIn pages.
