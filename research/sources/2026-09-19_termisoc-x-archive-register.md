# TermiSoc X/Twitter archive register

## Purpose

This register records the 19 September 2026 preservation and publication work for the historical official TermiSoc X/Twitter account. It is the single index for the account review, direct post evidence, attached media, third-party replies, and the public wiki presentation.

## Account and access

- Account: **TermiSoc**, `@TermiSoc`, <https://x.com/TermiSoc>.
- Public self-description: “The Plymouth University Computing and Technology Society.”
- Profile details observed: Plymouth, UK; linked `termisoc.org`; joined August 2008; 290 displayed posts.
- Access: the project maintainer authenticated to their own X account and authorised read-only historical review and preservation. No posts, replies, follows, likes, reposts, bookmarks, account settings, or messages were created or changed.
- Limits: X rendered an initial timeline and a Photos view reporting 22 photos and videos, but exposed 20 historic photo-post URLs. This is not a complete 290-post export, and the difference remains unresolved.

## Handling rule

For any additional X conversation material:

1. Preserve a research copy and a manifest entry first, including posting account, status URL, parent status URL, file properties, and SHA-256 hash.
2. Attribute any reply text and media to the account that posted it.
3. Copy to `source/Media/` and embed in a public page only when the project maintainer authorises public use and the context is historically relevant.
4. Do not infer people, event names, venues, or rights from an image alone.
