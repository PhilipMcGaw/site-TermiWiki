# TermiSoc Facebook-group description and committee, September 2014

## Source and retrieval

The project maintainer supplied transcriptions of the official TermiSoc Facebook-group description dated 8 and 28 September 2014 on 17 September 2026. Both call themselves the official group of TermiSoc and link to `http://www.termisoc.org`.

## Direct evidence

| Role | Name |
| --- | --- |
| Chairman | Matt Farrell |
| Secretary | Robbie Rowe |
| Treasurer | Jacob Gathercole |
| Safety Officer | George Coveny (8 September); George Coveney (28 September) |

## Interpretation

The two descriptions provide direct evidence that the same committee was displayed at least from 8 to 28 September 2014. They supply the missing dated context for Jacob Gathercole’s earlier Treasurer role and show Matt Farrell as Chairman before the later 2015 and 2016 committee records.

The Safety Officer’s surname is spelled **Coveny** in the 8 September transcription and **Coveney** in the 28 September transcription. The project already has a George Coveney page, and the one-letter difference is a likely typo, but it should not be silently normalised in archival transcriptions.

It does not establish the exact start/end dates of any officer’s term, whether the description had been updated promptly, or the relationship of the listed committee to academic-year elections.

## Publication boundary

The source is a private Facebook group. Retain this record in `research/`; do not reproduce the named committee list in the public vault without explicit project-maintainer approval.
