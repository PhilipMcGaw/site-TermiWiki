# TermiSoc emergency safety rulings, March 2015

## Source and retrieval

The project maintainer supplied a transcription of a TermiSoc private Facebook-group post dated 20 March 2015 on 17 September 2026. The post says that an emergency executive meeting had agreed two new rulings.

## Direct evidence

1. Unapproved extension cables were prohibited, including multi-socket/Y-plug adaptors.
2. Trolleys not previously approved by the Health and Safety Officer were prohibited on campus.

## Interpretation

This establishes that the TermiSoc executive had introduced specific safety controls by 20 March 2015. It does not give the incident, venue, event type, precise meeting attendees, or duration of the rules, so none should be inferred.

The timing aligns with the 22 March 2015 executive graphic, which labels Harriet Eldred as Health and Safety. That alignment supports, but does not independently prove, that she was the officer responsible for trolley approvals when the rulings were adopted.

## Publication boundary

The evidence comes from a private group and stays in `research/`. Do not publish the post text or identify a causal incident without explicit approval and independent context.
