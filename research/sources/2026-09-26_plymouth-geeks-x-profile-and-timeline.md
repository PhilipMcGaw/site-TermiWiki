# Plymouth Geeks public X/Twitter profile and timeline

## Scope and access

On 26 September 2026, the public profile at <https://x.com/PlymouthGeeks> was reviewed in X's browser interface while signed in to the project maintainer's account. The account's public posts and profile fields were read only; no follow, like, reply, repost, bookmark, message, or other account action was taken. This is a curated sample of historically relevant content, not a complete export.

X displayed **230 posts** and the timeline loaded posts dated **21 September 2011 back to 12 April 2010** in this session. Further scrolling did not load older posts. These are the bounds of the material observed in this pass, not a claim that the account's full history begins or ends on those dates.

## Profile as displayed

- Display name: **Plymouth Geek Meetup**
- Handle: **@plymouthgeeks**
- Bio: “trying to create a better geek community in plymouth-join us!”
- Location: **plymouth, uk**
- Joined: **April 2009**
- Displayed counts: **230 posts**, **58 following**, **214 followers**

The follower/following and post counts are volatile profile details recorded as displayed on the retrieval date. X's profile page is the source for these details.[^profile]

## Interpretation and confidence

- **High:** On 26 September 2026, X displayed the profile name, handle, bio, location, joined date, counts, status text, dates, and status links recorded above.
- **High:** The account directly promoted TermiSoc activities in 2010 and 2011, and directly invited `@TermiSoc` to a DCLUG meeting. This establishes interaction and cross-promotion, not organisational identity or shared membership.
- **Medium:** Plymouth Geeks was operating a public community with in-person meetups by April 2010. The account's own contemporaneous announcements are direct evidence of planned activity; independent corroboration of meeting attendance and outcomes is still limited.
- **Unverified:** Founders, membership, logo design origin, formal relationship to TermiSoc, and whether any announced event occurred as planned.
- **Unverified:** Whether the timeline items available in this session represent a substantial or complete portion of the 230 posts displayed by X.
- **Unverified:** Whether Internet Archive holds captures of the profile or timeline; the failed index lookups above do not prove that captures are absent.
