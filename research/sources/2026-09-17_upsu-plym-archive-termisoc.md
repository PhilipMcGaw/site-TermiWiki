# TermiSoc pages on the former UPSU Plymouth domain

## Source and retrieval

The Wayback Machine URL index for `http://www.upsu.plym.ac.uk/` was reviewed on 17 September 2026. It contains 2,503 captured URLs and 17 URLs matching “termisoc”. The homepage itself has 96 captures between 10 January 1998 and 1 June 2014.

## Direct evidence

- Official UPSU-hosted TermiSoc executive pages survive for 1996–97, 1997–98, and 1998–99.
- The 1996–97 page identifies Jason Gould as President, Alex Stanley as Secretary, Steve Sloggett as Treasurer, and Alex McLean as Technical Officer. It also says that a female Public Relations Officer had just been elected, without naming her.
- The 1997–98 page identifies Jeff Powell (President), Alex Charrett (Secretary), Aaron Trevena (Treasurer), Keith Langmead (Technical Officer), Gareth Alden (PR Officer), and Ross Childs (Projects Officer).
- The 1998–99 page identifies Jon Still (President), Rob Callaghan (Secretary), Pete Hatton (Treasurer), Keith Langmead (Technical Officer), Tiley Marner (PR Officer), and Alex Charrett (Projects Officer).
- A contemporaneously archived constitution calls the organisation **The Terminal Society (TermiSoc)**. Its stated objects include member webspace and email, technical help, support, HTML and Unix education, outings, and social events. It specifies President, Secretary, Treasurer, Technical Officer, and PR Officer as committee roles, with annual spring-term AGM elections.
- An archived “Chas’ Recent History” page supplies additional contemporary narrative for 1996–98, including the role transitions during 1996, the 1997 and 1998 AGMs, the acquisition of the `prot` and `esra` machines, and the claimed approximately 60 new members at the 1997 Freshers’ Fayre.

## Interpretation

These official-host captures corroborate the corresponding migrated executive and constitution pages and establish strong provenance for them. The historical narrative is contemporaneous but authored and informal; its claims beyond named officers should remain attributed to the page rather than treated as independently verified.
