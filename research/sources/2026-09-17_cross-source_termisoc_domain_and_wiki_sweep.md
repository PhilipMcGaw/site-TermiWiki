# Cross-source TermiSoc domain and wiki sweep

## Scope

Cross-check of the historical `termisoc.org` web-space evidence against the current TermiWiki research corpus and independent indexed historical sources. The aim was to identify pages, infrastructure, people, and subjects not already represented in the corpus.

## New or strengthened findings

### TermiSoc Time

Independent surviving reference material describes **TermiSoc Time** as a time system devised by the University of Plymouth Computing Society. The surviving description distinguishes it from Swatch Internet Time by using UTC rather than Biel Time and dividing the day into 1,024 units described as bytes.

Sources:

- https://fr-academic.com/dic.nsf/frwiki/777130/
- https://new-country-projects.fandom.com/wiki/Swatch_Internet_Time
- https://www.techno-science.net/definition/3889.html

Interpretation: this is a potentially publishable TermiSoc subject, but the surviving sources are secondary/reference copies rather than a contemporaneous TermiSoc primary source. Do not yet present the exact implementation details as independently primary evidence.

Confidence: Medium.

### TermiSoc infrastructure

The historical web-space model is further supported by independent references to:

- `termisoc.org/~betty` — repeatedly used by Aaron Trevena in 2001.
- `termisoc.org/~moose/tutorials` — David Pithouse's historical tutorial material.
- `termisoc.org/~j/files/example.xls` and `SWdata.xls` — files linked from a 2003 forum discussion.
- `termisoc.org/~andyk/chemfan/` — ChemFan web and mailing-list material.
- `termisoc.org/~harl/tranqs.html` — page later preserved by Erowid.

Sources:

- https://www.mail-archive.com/search?f=1&l=london-pm%40lists.dircon.co.uk&o=newest&q=from%3A%22Aaron+Trevena%22
- https://www.slideserve.com/matteo/mysql-and-php-tutorial-powerpoint-ppt-presentation
- https://www.pcreview.co.uk/threads/search-and-replace-9000-times.958365/
- https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Chemical_Information_Sources/SIRCh/Chemistry_Blogs_and_Discussion_Groups/Listserves%2C_Discussion_Lists%2C_and_Newsgroups_for_Chemistry
- https://www.erowid.org/chemicals/barbiturates/barbiturates_info2.shtml

Interpretation: the evidence strongly supports TermiSoc providing multi-user personal web space and hosting specialist projects/material. It does not yet establish the complete namespace or identify every username.

Confidence: High for existence of the multi-user namespace; Low for completeness.

### Additional person lead

**Connor Baxter** is independently indexed as having attended the University of Plymouth from 2013–2016 and lists Termisoc among his university activities/societies.

Source:

- https://uk.linkedin.com/in/connor-baxter-341108a6

This is a current retrospective profile rather than contemporaneous evidence. It should therefore be treated as a person-identification lead rather than strong historical evidence until corroborated.

Confidence: Medium for identity chronology; Medium for TermiSoc association based on the explicit profile statement.

## Negative findings / limits

Searches for combinations of newly identified people with `termisoc.org/~` did not produce reliable username mappings in the current indexed corpus. In particular, no new mapping was established for Jason Hill, Dominic Norton, Edward Knapp, Christopher Jenkins, Joshua von Eicken, Matthew Weikert, Matt Farrell, Edward McCaughan, or Connor Baxter.

This is a negative search result only and must not be interpreted as evidence that these people did not have TermiSoc web spaces.

The search also did not establish a complete enumeration of the historical `~username` namespace. A true CDX/Common Crawl/Memento enumeration remains a separate task.

## Research leads

1. Search `termisoc.org` and historical subdomains for exact filenames and directory references associated with each known person.
2. Search mailing-list archives for signatures containing `@termisoc.org` and `termisoc.org/~`.
3. Search historical external links for `termisoc.org/~` rather than relying only on the Wayback interface.
4. Investigate TermiSoc Time for a contemporaneous primary source, especially old TermiSoc pages, mailing lists, or member pages.
5. Attempt systematic archive enumeration of `termisoc.org/~*` using Wayback CDX, Common Crawl indexes, and Memento when direct access is available.
