# Ben A'Lee historical Subvert sites

## What was investigated

The supplied malformed Wayback URL was interpreted as a lead for the `subvert.org.uk` domain and Ben A'Lee's possible historical personal sites.

## Direct evidence

- Debian Ikiwiki users documentation explicitly lists **Ben A'Lee homepage** at `http://subvert.org.uk/~bma/` and his **wiki** at `http://wiki.subvert.org.uk/`.[^1]
- A Debian legal mailing-list message dated 25 February 2009 is from **Benjamin M. A'Lee**, uses a `bma-lists@subvert.org.uk` address, originates from `lennon.subvert.org.uk`, and signs off with the homepage URL.[^2]
- Open-source project archives independently associate Benjamin A'Lee with the same domain through `bma@subvert.org.uk` and `bma+lists-AT-subvert.org.uk` addresses.[^3]

## Interpretation

The evidence supports recording `subvert.org.uk/~bma/` as one of Ben A'Lee's historical personal sites and `wiki.subvert.org.uk` as an associated personal wiki. Domain association: **High** confidence.

The evidence does not establish exact dates of operation, site contents, ownership of the parent domain, or a specific Internet Archive capture. The supplied Wayback URL is incomplete because it contains no capture timestamp.
