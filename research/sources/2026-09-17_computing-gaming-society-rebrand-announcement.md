# Computing & Gaming Society re-brand announcement

## Source and retrieval

The user supplied a transcription of an 11 November 2016 TermiSoc Facebook-group post by Matt Farrell, headed **Computing & Gaming Society**, on 17 September 2026. The post links to the former UPSU society page:

<https://www.upsu.com/societies/CGS/>

## Direct evidence

- The post promotes a limited period of free membership under the name **Computing & Gaming Society** (CGS).
- It describes CGS as a long-running university society, welcomes interests in computing, gaming, hacking, and general technology, and advertises fortnightly workshops/talks, weekly socials, and LAN parties.
- The post appeared in the historical TermiSoc Facebook group, shortly before the December 2016 LAN event whose description also uses the CGS name.
- The project maintainer identifies CGS as a re-brand of TermiSoc.

The user also supplied the TermiSoc Facebook-group description dated **26 October 2016**. It calls itself “The official group of the computing and gaming society (formerly TermiSoc)” and lists this committee:

| Role | Name |
| --- | --- |
| Chairman | Jacob Gathercole |
| Secretary | Rachael Tordoff |
| Treasurer | Matt Farrell |
| Safety Officer | Chris Brown |

## Interpretation

Together, the dated group description, the dated post, their placement in the TermiSoc group, the December 2016 event, and the maintainer's identification provide high-confidence evidence that CGS was TermiSoc's operating name by 26 October 2016. The group description is direct contemporary evidence for the listed committee roles on that date. The post's claim that it was “one of the longest running societies” is promotional self-description, not independent historical proof.
