# Plymouth Geeks Ning website

## Scope and retrieval

On 26 September 2026, the historic address <http://plymouthgeeks.ning.com> was checked in both HTTP and HTTPS forms using the web retrieval interface. Neither was accessible. Exact-domain and name-plus-Ning searches returned no relevant indexed copy. Internet Archive CDX queries for the subdomain and matching Ning URLs were also inaccessible through the retrieval interface. These failures describe the tools available in this lookup; they do not establish that the site or archived captures are absent.

## Direct evidence

- A contemporaneous Plymouth Geeks X post dated 12 April 2010 directed users to the group's Ning forums and events site.[^ning]
- A second post dated 16 April 2010 said Ning appeared to be going non-free and that the group expected to move its website again.[^move]
- The X timeline review recorded these posts from the account's rendered public timeline. Its related source record identifies the status URLs and dates.[^timeline]

The posts establish how the Plymouth Geeks account described and used the Ning address. They do not expose the Ning site's actual pages, membership, event listings, or forum discussions.

## Interpretation and confidence

- **High:** The Plymouth Geeks account publicly directed users to a Ning-hosted forum/events presence by 12 April 2010. This is based on its contemporaneous post, as recorded in the reviewed timeline.
- **Medium:** The group expected to leave Ning in April 2010 because of a change to Ning's pricing. The post directly reports the account's understanding and intention; it does not establish the platform's precise pricing change or when a migration occurred.
- **Unverified:** The site's page structure, contents, launch date, membership, discussion history, replacement website, and whether any archived captures survive.
- **Unverified:** The present ownership or availability of the subdomain. An inaccessible page in this lookup is not proof of expiry or deletion.
