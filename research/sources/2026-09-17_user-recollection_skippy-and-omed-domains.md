# Source

Firsthand recollection supplied by Philip McGaw during the TermiWiki archaeology project on 17 September 2026.

## Direct evidence / recollection

Philip McGaw states that:

- `skippy.org.uk` was hosted by TermiSoc a very long time ago.
- `omed.co.uk` or `omeds.co.uk` was formerly associated with him.

These statements are recorded as firsthand recollection, not as independently verified archival evidence.

## Independent corroboration found during the same investigation

An Academic Kids page for “Skippy” identifies `Skippy.org.uk` as the nickname/site of Philip McGaw and explicitly states that he was a member of TermiSoc. This is secondary evidence and does not itself establish that the site was hosted on TermiSoc infrastructure. Source: https://academickids.com/encyclopedia/index.php/Skippy

A 2016 Postfix mailing-list discussion independently demonstrates that `skippy.org.uk` was operated by Philip McGaw by that date, including mail delivery and the hostname `njoror` in the local mail logs. Source: https://groups.google.com/g/mailing.postfix.users/c/BdWWJCIi6Og

## Interpretation

The recollection makes `skippy.org.uk` a particularly valuable target for historical infrastructure archaeology. If an archived page, mail header, DNS record, WHOIS record, or configuration fragment links `skippy.org.uk` to `termisoc.org`, it could provide a direct bridge between a known TermiSoc member's personal namespace and the society's historical hosting infrastructure.

The `omed.co.uk` / `omeds.co.uk` recollection is recorded as a separate domain-history lead. No independent TermiSoc connection has yet been established for either domain.

## Open questions

1. Which of `omed.co.uk` and `omeds.co.uk` was actually used, and during what period?
2. When did `skippy.org.uk` first exist?
3. What IP address(es) did `skippy.org.uk` resolve to during the period when Philip recalls TermiSoc hosting it?
4. Can those IP address(es) be matched to a historical `termisoc.org` host, University of Plymouth address space, or one of the three servers described by Keith Langmead?
5. Did the historical DNS records use a CNAME to a TermiSoc hostname rather than an A record directly on `skippy.org.uk`?

## Publication impact

Do not state as independently established fact that TermiSoc hosted `skippy.org.uk` until archival or infrastructure evidence is recovered. The firsthand recollection should, however, guide targeted DNS, Wayback, mail-header, and hostname searches.
