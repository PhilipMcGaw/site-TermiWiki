# Public search for WhatsApp administrator numbers

## Investigation

Four telephone numbers supplied by a project contributor as WhatsApp group administrator numbers were checked against indexed public pages on official University of Plymouth, UPSU, and identified CompSoc-related domains.

The numbers are not reproduced here because they are personal contact data.

## Search and retrieval

- Search date: 2026-09-18.
- Search scope: exact-number searches restricted to `upsu.com`, `plymouth.ac.uk`, and `compsoc.co.uk`.
- Result: no indexed matches were returned for any of the four numbers.

## What this establishes

- No public listing was found in the searched official-domain scope: **Medium confidence**.
- This does not establish that the numbers are not published elsewhere, are not associated with private profiles, or are not visible to logged-in users: **High confidence**.
