# TermiSoc photograph batch, September-November 2005

## Source and retrieval

The project maintainer supplied 19 original JPEG files on 20 September 2026. Their camera metadata identifies a Nikon D70 and records creation times from 22 September to 25 November 2005. The files, dimensions, and SHA-256 checksums are preserved in the [research-media directory](../media/2026-09-20_user-supplied-termisoc-photographs/README.md).

The batch includes identifiable people. It is retained for research, not embedded in `source/` or generated output. Do not assign names from facial appearance, or publish individual portraits, without consent and a specific editorial decision.

## Direct evidence

### Sports & Societies Fair — 29 September 2005

The eleven files in this sequence are associated with the dedicated [TermiSoc Sports & Societies Fair recruitment event record](../events/2026-09-20_termisoc-sports-societies-fair-2005.md).

`DSC_6088.JPG`, `DSC_6091.JPG`, `DSC_6092.JPG`, `DSC_6177.JPG`, `DSC_6179.JPG`, `DSC_6185.JPG`, `DSC_6193.JPG`, `DSC_6321.JPG`, `DSC_6322.JPG`, `DSC_6325.JPG`, and `DSC_6327.JPG` form a sequence dated 29 September 2005.

They directly show a TermiSoc stand with a projection, printed promotional sheets, TermiSoc-branded clothing, and a penguin mascot. The visible materials state:

- “500MB of webspace, spamproof e-mail and your own web address for £5?”
- “Call it a student discount.”
- “See us at the Sports & Societies Fair, Main Hall, table 42.”
- “Because some people prefer to frag grunts than go surfing.”

The sequence establishes that TermiSoc promoted a £5 membership/service offer including 500 MB of web space, spam-proof email, and a personal web address at the named fair. It does not establish how long the offer ran, how many people joined, or the names and roles of people present.

## Interpretation and limits

The images provide strong event and service-offer evidence because the relevant text is contemporaneous and visibly present in a tightly dated sequence. The exact event label comes from the photographed sheets. Camera metadata corroborates, but does not independently prove, the capture date.

No identity matches, organisational positions, ownership rights beyond the maintainer's deposit, or consent to publish the people pictured are established by this record.
