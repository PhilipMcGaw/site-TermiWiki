## Direct evidence

### Committee flyer

The flyer describes TermiSoc as the Plymouth University Computing and Technology Society. It lists the following main executive members:

- Chairman — Matt Farrell
- Treasurer — Jacob Gathercole
- Secretary — Robbie Rowe
- Social Secretary — Lucy Farrell
- Health and Safety — George Coveney

It also advertises weekly workshops and socials, hackathons, projects, LAN parties jointly with GameSoc, movie nights, a £4 membership fee, and a main-hall location.

### First 24 Hour LAN flyer

The flyer announces TermiSoc's “First 24 Hour LAN” as coming in October 2014. It advertises planned tournaments including Team Fortress 2, Mario Kart, Supreme Commander, and Magic: The Gathering, and says that Monday meetings were held in Babbage at 19:00.

The flyer is direct evidence that the event was announced. It does not by itself establish its exact date, venue, or that it took place.

## Supporting photograph

The research-only photograph shows a stack of printed copies of the committee flyer beside a laptop. Its visible system date is contextual only and is not treated as independent proof of when the flyer was made or distributed.

## Scope and open questions

- The flyers support the recorded committee list and the planned October 2014 LAN.
- The relationship between this committee list and later role recollections requires chronological reconciliation; no inference has been made from private correspondence.
- Confirm the exact event date, venue and outcome through a dated post, event record, or further contemporaneous material.
