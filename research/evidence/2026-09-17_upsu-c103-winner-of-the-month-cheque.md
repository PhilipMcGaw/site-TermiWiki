## Interpretation

The cheque is direct evidence that an entity styled “UPSU C103” received a £75 Winner of the Month award in January 2006. The related lettering photograph provides contemporaneous evidence of a TermiSoc association with C103 as a physical location. Together, they are a useful lead on a University of Plymouth Students’ Union-connected activity, but they do not establish what C103 was, whether it was a formal TermiSoc project, or why the award was made.
