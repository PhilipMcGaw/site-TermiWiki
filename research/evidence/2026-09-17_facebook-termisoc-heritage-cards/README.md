# TermiSoc heritage membership cards

## Source and retrieval

An image posted by Deric Toms to the private TermiSoc Facebook group on 9 September 2017, retrieved in a user-authorised session on 17 September 2026:

<https://www.facebook.com/photo/?fbid=10159362741885192&set=gm.10154966708873785>

The post caption is “Heritage membership cards. Not in the same league as the carrot leaflet.”

## Direct evidence

- The image shows six historic UPSU Club/Society Member cards held by Deric Toms.
- The cards include society names written as `Terminal`, `Termisoc`, and `Terminal Society`, alongside cards for other societies.
- This is direct material evidence for multiple naming forms of the society on UPSU membership cards.

## Interpretation

The cards are strong historical artefacts, but they include the member's name, signature, and membership-number details. They are therefore retained as **research-only** evidence, not published in the source vault.
