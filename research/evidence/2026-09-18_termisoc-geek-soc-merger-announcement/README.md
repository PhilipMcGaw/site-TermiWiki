# TermiSoc merger into Geek Soc announcement

## Source and retrieval

On 18 September 2026, the project contributor supplied a screenshot recovered by Matt Farrell of the TermiSoc announcement concerning the society's merger into Geek Soc. Matt subsequently supplied the announcement text directly to the contributor. The linked Facebook event is **Geek-Soc AGM**: <https://www.facebook.com/events/894845040655489>.

The screenshot is preserved as research-only provenance. It is not a substitute for an independently retrievable Facebook post or University/Students' Union record.

## Direct evidence

The announcement states that TermiSoc was no longer going forward, that it had “merged into geek soc”, and that Geek Soc would take over sorting members. It says existing paid members would receive free membership, directs readers to Geek Soc's AGM, and describes a planned Tech Officer role involving LANs, equipment and online servers such as Minecraft.

The statement ends by saying that the legacy would continue. It provides direct, contemporaneous wording for the February 2017 transition and confirms that Geek Soc was presented as the destination for both membership and remaining activities.

> Hi everyone,
>
> Sorry for the lack of communication to start. Secondly and more importantly mixed news.
>
> The sad news is we realised there isn't place for us in the university any more and it's this realisation that means we are no longer going forward.

They cover a lot of the areas termisoc did they are social, they enjoy geeky movies, they play console and pc games and much more. As we have merged we are sorting out a way to move our members but as su is a pain it will be via a free membership for all our paid members.
>
> Now lastly as su is slow it means it will be after geek soc's agm but anyone who wants to run for a position should attend, vote and take part. A new position will be open called tech officer, many of the old termisoc alumni know about this position for the newer guys it's a position that creates, manages and runs the LAN's, hold the equipment and runs any online servers like minecraft.
>
> Here is the link to the AGM event, <https://www.facebook.com/events/894845040655489>
>
> We are sorry we took over and we're the last committee of the society but good news the legacy will continue.

Matt explains that the trailing screenshot lines, “Login to su website send this email to our members. Are you free now too?”, concern recovery of a draft email after losing access to his University account. They are not part of the announcement and are excluded from this transcription.

## Interpretation

This supports the existing public description of Geek Society as the society into which TermiSoc merged in February 2017. It does **not** establish a simple rename or uninterrupted institutional continuity: the announcement describes a merger and successor membership arrangement.
