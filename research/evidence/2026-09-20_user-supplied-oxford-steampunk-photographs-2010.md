# User-supplied Oxford steampunk-event photographs, 2010

## Source and handling

The project maintainer supplied four original photographs on 20 September 2026 and described them as photographs from a steampunk event in Oxford. The preserved originals and their checksums are held in the [research-media directory](../media/2026-09-20_user-supplied-oxford-steampunk-photographs-2010/README.md).

The images contain identifiable people. They are retained as research media only: do not identify people from visual resemblance, infer affiliations, or publish captions without explicit confirmation and consent.

## Direct evidence

- All four files retain camera creation times on 16 January 2010, between 12:25:46 and 14:05:49.
- The photographs show people wearing period-inspired formal and steampunk-style clothing in an indoor venue.
- `IMG_0389.jpg` directly records a sign for “Major Tinker's Emporium”, with the wording “The Very Finest Notions & Trinkets For the Discerning Steampunk” and the URL `http://tinkers-emporium.synthasite.com`.
- The project maintainer identifies **Ross “Sayyan” Bearman** in `IMG_0385.jpg`, **Christa “PixelChu” Briggs** in `IMG_0386.jpg`, and **Ben A'Lee** (`bma`) in `IMG_0387.jpg`. These are maintainer-supplied identifications and are not inferences from visual resemblance; the second attendee in `IMG_0385.jpg` remains unidentified.

## Interpretation

The maintainer's first-hand description places the event in Oxford and characterises it as steampunk. The visible attire and vendor sign are consistent with that account. The photographs do not establish the event's formal title, precise venue, organiser, or a connection to TermiSoc.

No existing Oxford or steampunk material was found in the repository at preservation time. This is therefore a standalone contextual record and does not support a curated `source/` update.
