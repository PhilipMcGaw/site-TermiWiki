## Interpretation

The labelled graphic establishes a dated seven-person TermiSoc committee at 22 March 2015. It corroborates the later October 2015 group-description roles for Woods, Milner, Akers, and Eldred, while adding the Technical Officer, BCS Liaison, and Events Secretary roles.

The two sources independently corroborate the five core names and roles. “Michael Burgess” in the second source is a plausible match for “Mike ‘Ducky’ Burgess” in the labelled March graphic, but it remains a name-variant lead rather than a confirmed identity bridge.

The graphic’s “Elle Milner” label is consistent with the independently verified group-member display name Eleanor Kate Milner, but the shortened form should be preserved as a label from this source rather than treated as the person’s formal name.

A private group post dated 20 March 2015 records new safety rulings requiring Health and Safety Officer approval for trolleys, two days before this image was posted. See [[../../sources/2026-09-17_termisoc-safety-rulings-march-2015|the safety-rulings record]].
