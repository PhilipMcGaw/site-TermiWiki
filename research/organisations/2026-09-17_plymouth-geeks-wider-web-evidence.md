# Plymouth Geeks wider-web evidence

## What was investigated

The existing TermiWiki page describes Plymouth Geeks as a TermiSoc-adjacent, non-university community and names the Twitter account `@plymouthgeeks`. Wider-web searches were used to test whether the community and account are independently visible.

## Direct evidence

- The DCLUG archive preserves a message dated 14 February 2010 with the subject “Re: [LUG] plymouth geeks meetup on the 18th of feb?”. The accessible search result preserves the subject and identifies the archive record, but the retrieved page was not available from cache for full inspection.[^1]
- An Ask MetaFilter discussion dated 4 March 2012 recommends `@PlymouthGeeks` as a source of local information about Plymouth. The page records the handle in a contemporaneous public discussion.[^2]

## Interpretation

- A Plymouth Geeks meetup or meetup discussion is directly evidenced by February 2010: **Medium** confidence. The message subject is clear, but the full message body and organiser are not currently verified.
- The `@PlymouthGeeks` account was publicly associated with Plymouth by March 2012: **Medium** confidence. The evidence is a third-party recommendation, not an account archive or self-authored profile.
- The existing description of Plymouth Geeks as a community involving former and current TermiSoc members remains **Unverified** from the wider-web sources found here.
- The relationship between this Plymouth Geeks community and the later University of Plymouth Geek Society is **Unverified**. They should remain separate unless direct evidence connects them.
