# UPSU Games Society and CompSoc pages

## Sources and retrieval

- [UPSU Games Society](https://www.upsu.com/societies/gamessociety/), retrieved 17 September 2026.
- [UPSU CompSoc (Computer Society)](https://www.upsu.com/societies/compsoc/), retrieved 17 September 2026.

## Direct evidence

The UPSU Games Society page describes a current tabletop-gaming society with trading-card, board-game, wargaming, and role-playing sections. It lists regular sessions, contact details, and committee information.

The UPSU CompSoc page describes the current Computer Society as a welcoming community for technology, creativity, coding, games, and problem-solving. It lists current committee roles, including Joshua Hutchinson as Safety Officer.

## Interpretation

These pages document two current University of Plymouth student societies. They are useful for the later institutional and social landscape around TermiWiki, but neither page establishes continuity with historical TermiSoc. Games Society and CompSoc should remain separate organisations in the archive.

## Confidence

- **Current existence and public descriptions of both societies:** High, based on direct UPSU pages.
- **Historical continuity with TermiSoc:** Unverified; no such claim is made.
