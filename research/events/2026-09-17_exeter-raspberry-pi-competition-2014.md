# Exeter Raspberry Pi competition promoted to TermiSoc, January 2014

## Source and retrieval

The project maintainer supplied a transcription of an Elina Lulle post in the private TermiSoc Facebook group, dated 8 January 2014. It links to the former Exeter Computer Science site `http://excompsci.co.uk/coding/`.

## Direct evidence

- The post promotes an Exeter Raspberry Pi competition for Sunday 19 January 2014, 10:00–22:00.
- Entries were to be entered into a “PA competition” and could win £50 on the day.
- The post says that a 48-hour Exeter hackathon in the prior term had been successful and that this was another event.
- It invites TermiSoc group members to sign up via the Exeter site.

## Interpretation

This is direct evidence of an Exeter computing-community event being promoted to TermiSoc members. It does not establish that TermiSoc organised, funded, attended, or formally partnered in the competition. “PA competition” is retained as written because its meaning is not established by the post.

## Public-archive checks (17 September 2026)

- The Internet Archive has no capture for the exact `http://excompsci.co.uk/coding/` URL.
- The root `http://excompsci.co.uk/` has one 2018 capture. It redirects to a generic parked-domain page at `exetercss.co.uk`, rather than preserving the Exeter Computer Science Society site or event material.
- PA Consulting's retrospective list records the University of Exeter as the undergraduate winner of its 2014 Raspberry Pi Competition. This independently establishes that an Exeter undergraduate entry won that year's PA competition, but does **not** establish that it was the event promoted in the TermiSoc post, nor that the Sunday event selected or represented that entry.

## Outcome

The original private-group post remains the only located source for the January 2014 event's date, hours, £50 local prize and the former `excompsci.co.uk/coding/` sign-up link. No public copy of that event page has been found.
