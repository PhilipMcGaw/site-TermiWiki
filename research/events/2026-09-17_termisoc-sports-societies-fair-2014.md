# TermiSoc Sports and Societies Fair recruitment, September 2014

## Source and retrieval

The project maintainer supplied a transcription of a Lucy Farrell post in the private TermiSoc Facebook group, dated 23 September 2014. The post promotes the following day’s Sports and Societies Fair and links to the former UPSU society page `http://www.upsu.com/societies/7017/`.

## Direct evidence

- The fair was scheduled for Wednesday 24 September 2014, 12:00–17:00.
- TermiSoc described itself as the “Plymouth Computing And Technology Society”.
- Annual membership was advertised at £4.
- The TermiSoc stall was stated to be in the Rolle Plaza Marquee, stand 52.
- The post advertised a free homemade mascot, “Nano the Termifuzz Mascot”, for people signing up at the stall.

## Interpretation

This is direct evidence of an active TermiSoc recruitment presence at the 2014 fair, its advertised membership price, and its contemporaneous Computing and Technology Society description. The mascot claim is an offer in promotional material; it does not establish how many were made or distributed.
