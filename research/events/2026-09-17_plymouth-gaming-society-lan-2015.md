# Plymouth University Gaming Society LAN, October 2015

## Source and retrieval

The project maintainer supplied a transcription of a post by Babatunde Benjamin in the private TermiSoc Facebook group, dated 29 September 2015. It promotes an upcoming Plymouth University Gaming Society LAN and links to the former UPSU event page:

<http://www.upsu.com/ents/event/3741/>

Wayback has no capture of the exact HTTP event URL. The date of the event is therefore inferred from the post’s “this Saturday” wording: 3 October 2015.

## Direct evidence

- The event was described as a LAN run by Plymouth University Gaming Society.
- A TermiSoc group member promoted it to the TermiSoc group on 29 September 2015.

## Interpretation

The post shows awareness and promotion of Gaming Society activity within the TermiSoc community. It does not establish that TermiSoc organised, sponsored, attended, or formally partnered with the LAN. The estimated 3 October date must remain marked as an inference until another event record is recovered.
