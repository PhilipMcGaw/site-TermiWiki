# Christmas 24hour LAN & Gaming Party — Facebook event evidence

## Source and retrieval

Public Facebook event, accessed in a user-authorised Facebook session on 17 September 2026:

<https://www.facebook.com/events/211941039245279/>

The event was also listed in the historical TermiSoc Facebook group's event archive.

## Direct evidence

- The event is titled **Christmas 24hour LAN & Gaming Party**.
- It ran from 15:00 on 17 December 2016 until 15:00 on 18 December 2016 in the Davy Building Main Hall.
- Facebook identifies the event as being by Matt Farrell and the TermiSoc Facebook group.
- Its description calls the organiser the “Computing & Gaming Society”, identifies collaboration with Game Soc and MTG Plymouth, and describes computer games, Magic: The Gathering, and board games.
- The listed admission price was £3.50 regardless of membership status; the description contrasts this with a usual £5 price.
- The event page displayed 36 responses at retrieval, comprising 11 marked as having attended and 25 interested.

## Interpretation

This is high-confidence evidence for a TermiSoc-group-hosted or co-hosted 24-hour LAN event in December 2016. The Facebook host is TermiSoc, while the description uses “Computing & Gaming Society”. A dated 11 November 2016 CGS membership announcement in the TermiSoc group, together with the project maintainer's identification, resolves the naming discrepancy; the exact formal basis remains unknown. See [[../sources/2026-09-17_computing-gaming-society-rebrand-announcement|the re-brand announcement record]].
