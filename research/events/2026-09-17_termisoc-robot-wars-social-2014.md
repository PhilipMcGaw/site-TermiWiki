# TermiSoc Robot Wars social, January 2014

## Source and retrieval

The project maintainer supplied a transcription of a Jack Connaughton post in the private TermiSoc Facebook group, dated 14 January 2014.

## Direct evidence

- The post announces that the week’s TermiSoc social would be a Robot Wars marathon.
- It was scheduled for Thursday 16 January 2014, with a 19:00 start.
- The organiser was Jack Connaughton.

## Privacy handling

The original post gives a private residential address and postcode. Those details are deliberately excluded from this record and must not be copied into the public vault, event pages, or media metadata.

## Interpretation

This is direct evidence of a small TermiSoc social activity in January 2014. It does not establish attendance, official Students’ Union booking status, or a recurring Robot Wars event series.
