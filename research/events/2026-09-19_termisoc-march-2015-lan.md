# TermiSoc 24-hour LAN, March 2015

## Source and retrieval

`LAN Information.docx` was posted by Eleanor Kate Milner to the private TermiSoc Facebook group on 18 March 2015, accompanied by a message describing it as the official information for that weekend's LAN. The authenticated download was preserved on 19 September 2026 at `research/media/2026-09-19_termisoc-facebook-files/LAN Information.docx`; SHA-256: `51581106c71747161f0aeac903d9ac9fc1b5dae42de0a1d9099942cf9a39e0cc`.

## Direct evidence

- The LAN was scheduled from 12:00 on **Saturday 21 March** to 12:00 on **Sunday 22 March**. In the context of the dated Facebook post, this is 2015.
- The document does not name a venue.
- Admission was £4 for TermiSoc members and alumni, and £5 for others. University members could attend; only TermiSoc members received the discount.
- The event had named technical cover from Phil Akers, Callum Trounce, or Jamie Woods. Named health-and-safety officers were Jamie Woods, Harriet Eldred, and George Coveney.
- It lists a tournament programme including Garry's Mod Prop Hunt, Urban Terror, Trackmania, Team Fortress 2, Mario Kart, Super Smash Bros, and Minecraft Hunger Games and build competitions, plus selected board games.
- A Magic: The Gathering prerelease was planned from 18:00 until between 23:00 and midnight; its players had free entry unless also taking part in the LAN.
- TermiSoc did not provide internet access, though the document says it was considering this for future LANs.

## Corroboration and interpretation

The named staff align with the dated 22 March 2015 “Meet your Execs!” graphic: Jamie Woods (Chairman), Eleanor/Elle Milner (Secretary), Phil Akers (Treasurer), Harriet Eldred (Health and Safety), Callum Trounce (Technical Officer), George Coveney (Safety Officer), and other executive members. The LAN document provides direct evidence for the event and its arrangements; the committee graphic independently corroborates the relevant contemporary roles.

The 20 March 2015 emergency safety-rulings post predates the LAN by one day and is consistent with the document's detailed safety controls. This alignment does not establish that a particular rule arose from this event.
