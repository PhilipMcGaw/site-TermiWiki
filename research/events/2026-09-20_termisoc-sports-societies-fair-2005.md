# TermiSoc Sports & Societies Fair recruitment, 29 September 2005

## Source and retrieval

The project maintainer supplied a sequence of original Nikon D70 photographs on 20 September 2026. Eleven files have contiguous capture times on 29 September 2005 and visibly show TermiSoc’s promotional stand. The original files, their metadata, and SHA-256 checksums are held in [the research-media deposit](../media/2026-09-20_user-supplied-termisoc-photographs/README.md).

The images contain identifiable people and remain research-only. They are not source assets or publication-ready images.

## Direct evidence

The sequence (`DSC_6088.JPG`, `DSC_6091.JPG`, `DSC_6092.JPG`, `DSC_6177.JPG`, `DSC_6179.JPG`, `DSC_6185.JPG`, `DSC_6193.JPG`, `DSC_6321.JPG`, `DSC_6322.JPG`, `DSC_6325.JPG`, and `DSC_6327.JPG`) shows:

- a TermiSoc recruitment stand at the **Sports & Societies Fair**, **Main Hall**, **table 42**;
- TermiSoc-branded clothing, a penguin mascot, a projection, and printed recruitment material;
- an offer of **500 MB** of web space, spam-proof email, and a personal web address for **£5**; and
- the promotional wording “Because some people prefer to frag grunts than go surfing.”

## Interpretation

This is a documented 2005 Freshers’/Sports & Societies Fair event, rather than merely a generic recruitment image. It provides direct evidence for the visible venue, table number, and advertised membership/service offer.

It does not establish the event’s official university title, total attendance, number of memberships sold, or the identities and roles of people present.
