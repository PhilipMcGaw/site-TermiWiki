# TermiSoc X/Twitter account associations

## Scope and method

On 19 September 2026, the project maintainer logged into their own X account and authorised a read-only review of the 32 accounts followed by the official TermiSoc account, <https://x.com/TermiSoc/following>. The associations below are based on X's visible display name and handle, cross-checked against independently documented TermiSoc people pages where available.

Being followed by TermiSoc is evidence of a public account relationship at the time of inspection. It does **not** establish when the follow began, a committee role, or the nature of the relationship.
