# Source

Web archaeology conducted 2026-09-17, focusing on Alex Charrett, University of Plymouth, TermiSoc, and the Devon & Cornwall Linux User Group.

## Direct evidence

- Alex Charrett's current LinkedIn profile identifies the person as being associated with the University of Plymouth from 1996–2000 and lists **TermiSoc** among activities and societies. The same profile shows later systems/cloud-oriented technical work and Sun Solaris certifications: Sun Certified Systems Engineer for Solaris 8 (November 2001) and Sun Certified Network Administrator for Solaris 8 (April 2002). Source: https://uk.linkedin.com/in/alexcharrett
- Linux Format's December 2000 LUG directory lists **Devon & Cornwall**, URL `www.lug.termisoc.org`, with **Alex Charrett** as contact. Source: https://linuxformat.com/includes/download.php?PDF=LXF10.complete.pdf
- Aaron Trevena's current personal site says the Devon & Cornwall Linux User Group was co-founded with **Alex Charrett in 1999**, as part of running the University of Plymouth Computing & Internet Society. Source: https://www.aarontrevena.co.uk/
- Aaron Trevena's LinkedIn profile independently describes Alex Charrett as his **TermiSoc colleague** and says they co-founded the Devon & Cornwall Linux User Group. Source: https://uk.linkedin.com/in/aarontrevena
- Existing TermiWiki corpus evidence places an **Alex** in the Sun Lab in 1997. This is later documentation/recollection rather than contemporaneous evidence and should not by itself be used to identify the first name with full certainty.
- An Arch Linux bug report from 2010 was opened by `Alex Charrett (acharrett)` and contains a technical report about Postgrey, Exim, and package dependencies. This is useful identity/technical-continuity evidence for the modern Alex Charrett, but is not TermiSoc-period evidence. Source: https://bugs.archlinux.org/task/18093

## Interpretation

The evidence strongly establishes Alex Charrett as a TermiSoc-associated University of Plymouth student in the 1996–2000 period and as an early participant/contact for the Devon & Cornwall Linux User Group. The 1999 co-founding account and the December 2000 Linux Format directory provide a useful bridge between TermiSoc and the regional Linux community.

The Solaris certifications in 2001–2002 are consistent with the Unix/Sun computing environment represented elsewhere in the corpus, but they do not prove that Alex administered the University Sun Lab or TermiSoc servers. No such claim should be made without direct evidence.

The Sun Lab page's reference to an 'Alex' in 1997 may be this Alex Charrett, but the page gives only the first name. Treat the identification as corroborated context, not direct identification, until another source links the full name to the photograph or event.

## Corroboration

- The Linux Format directory is contemporary to the period and directly associates Alex Charrett with the TermiSoc-hosted `lug.termisoc.org` address.
- Aaron Trevena's later first-person account independently names Alex as his TermiSoc colleague and LUG co-founder.
- Alex's current LinkedIn profile independently gives the 1996–2000 University of Plymouth chronology and TermiSoc association.
