# RoboSoc person archaeology — TermiSoc-relevant profiles

## Scope

This record captures people identified during the University of Plymouth Robotics Society / RoboSoc sweep who provide useful links to the TermiSoc network or to the historical robotics society. LinkedIn activity/society statements are retrospective self-reports and are treated as identity and association evidence rather than contemporaneous institutional records.

The existence of a historical RoboSoc is supported by these profiles, but this record does **not** establish that the historical RoboSoc and the present University of Plymouth Robotics Society are formally continuous organisations.
