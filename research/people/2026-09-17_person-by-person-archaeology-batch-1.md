# TermiSoc person-by-person archaeology — batch 1

## Source

A targeted public-web search was conducted on 2026-09-17 using the firsthand-identified TermiSoc member list recorded in `research/sources/2026-09-17_firsthand_termisoc_member_identifications.md` as the search anchor. Searches included exact names, Plymouth, University of Plymouth, computing, TermiSoc/Termisoc, and where useful personal websites or professional profiles.

This record captures documentary evidence found during this pass. The project owner's firsthand identification remains a separate evidence class and is not silently upgraded by matching names online.

## Direct evidence

### Ben A'Lee

A 2006 FreeBSD mailing-list message by Benjamin A'Lee identifies the author as `Benjamin A'Lee`, gives `benalee.co.uk` as his homepage, and explicitly signs off with `Termisoc Tech Officer: http://termisoc.org/`. This is strong contemporaneous documentary evidence linking Ben A'Lee to TermiSoc and specifically to the Tech Officer role.

Source: FreeBSD questions mailing list, 18 February 2006.

URL: https://lists.freebsd.org/pipermail/freebsd-questions/2006-February/113401.html

Confidence: **High** for TermiSoc association and Tech Officer role in 2006.

## Interpretation

This pass materially strengthens the documentary people register. The strongest independent evidence currently found is for Ben A'Lee, Ross Bearman, Nick Charlton, Joe Earlam and Siddharth Vadgama. Nick Charlton's personal website is particularly valuable because it preserves contemporaneous first-person descriptions of TermiSoc activities rather than merely listing membership retrospectively.

The search also demonstrates that current professional profiles can preserve useful society membership and office information, but they vary considerably in evidential value. A current profile should be treated as a retrospective source unless it preserves a contemporaneous dated statement.

The absence of public results for a person is not evidence that the person was not involved with TermiSoc. It is recorded only as an unresolved documentary gap.

## TermiHouse relevance

No direct TermiHouse 1 evidence was found in this batch. The strongest next searches should combine the known people with `TermiHouse`, `Termi House`, house names/addresses, and archived personal pages. Nick Charlton's contemporaneous TermiSoc material and Ben A'Lee's historical personal-site footprint are particularly promising routes into the wider web archive.
