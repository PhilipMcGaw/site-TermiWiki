# Georgie Aggett — 2011 software-project lead

## Source

A targeted public-web search was conducted on 2026-09-17 following the project owner's identification of Georgie Aggett as a person worth investigating. Searches covered the exact name with Plymouth, University of Plymouth, computing, TermiSoc, and the names of known TermiSoc-era people.

## Direct evidence

Nick Charlton's personal website contains a first-person post, `Where's Next? Now In the App Store`, published **12 August 2011**. The post describes his iOS application *Where's Next?* and thanks three people who tested it and provided feedback: **Chris Hunt, Luke Davies and Georgie Aggett**.

Source: Nick Charlton, `Where's Next? Now In the App Store`, 12 August 2011.

URL: https://nickcharlton.net/posts/wheres-next-now-in-the-app-store

The same source describes the project as Nick's first iOS project, developed over many months, and says he had begun it before the start of 2011. The reference to Georgie is therefore contemporaneous with a software-development project in Nick's Plymouth-era network.

## Interpretation

This is a materially better lead than the earlier exact-name searches, which produced no useful public evidence. It establishes a **2011 social/technical association between Georgie Aggett and Nick Charlton**, whose University of Plymouth and TermiSoc roles are independently documented elsewhere in the corpus.

It does **not** establish Georgie Aggett as a TermiSoc member. The post does not mention TermiSoc, and the connection should not be upgraded by association with Nick.

The other two testers named in the same post are **Chris Hunt** and **Luke Davies**. Both are potentially useful person-discovery leads because they share the same contemporaneous software-development context. This does not, by itself, establish either person's TermiSoc involvement.

## Corroboration

Nick Charlton is independently documented as a University of Plymouth student from 2009–2013 and as TermiSoc Safety Officer (2010–2011) and Tech Officer (2011–2013). His own 2011 site also contains direct TermiSoc posts. Those facts make his 2011 software-development network particularly useful for further person archaeology, but they do not convert his contacts into TermiSoc members.

## Open questions

1. Can Georgie Aggett be independently connected to the University of Plymouth during or around 2011?
2. Is there an archived personal website, GitHub account, LinkedIn profile, portfolio, or other historical identity that matches this Georgie Aggett?
3. Does Georgie appear in archived TermiSoc event material, photographs, mailing-list messages, committee lists, or project pages?
4. Can Chris Hunt and Luke Davies be identified independently and used as parallel routes into the 2011 TermiSoc/software-development network?
5. Does the original `Where's Next?` project survive elsewhere with contributors, testers, source code, screenshots, or acknowledgements?

## Publication impact

Keep Georgie Aggett as an unresolved person lead. The evidence supports a dated association with Nick Charlton's software project, not TermiSoc membership. Any future TermiSoc claim should be based on separate documentary evidence or explicitly attributed firsthand recollection.
