# Theo Z and Theo Zourzouvillys identity comparison

## Repository evidence

The repository contains two person pages:

- [[source/TermiPeople/theo_z|Theo Z]], a stub used by the historical narrative.
- [[source/TermiPeople/theo_zourzouvillys|Theo Zourzouvillys]], which records
  2002–2003 Secretary, describes him as “The Crazy Greek”, and notes that he
  helped revive Area51 and Esra.

The 2002 sections of [[source/the_abridged_history|the abridged history]] name
“Z” as Secretary and describe Ian Christian and Theo bringing Area51 and Esra
back to life. The executive chronology separately records Zourzouvillys as
Secretary and Head Technical Officer for 2002–2003. The archived-member
research also identifies the 2002 involvement of “Ian Christian and Theo Z”.

## Public corroboration

An exact-name public search found a coherent technical identity under the rare
surname:

- [Theo Zourzouvillys — LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/zourzouvillys)
  is indexed with a long software-engineering and technology-leadership career,
  including Jive/LogMeIn, Auth0, and FluentStream.
- [Theo Zourzouvillys — personal site](https://zrz.io/) uses the exact full
  name and describes software engineering work.
- [IETF profile](https://datatracker.ietf.org/person/theo%40crazygreek.co.uk)
  and [RFC 6026](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6026) associate the full
  name with the long-standing `crazygreek.co.uk` identity and Skype-era VoIP
  standards work.
- A Companies House-derived record lists **Theo Peter Zourzouvillys** as a
  director of Associated Networks (UK) Limited from 4 December 2002 to 15
  April 2004, matching the TermiSoc page’s 2002–2003 period.

## Current domain status

The current [crazygreek.co.uk](https://crazygreek.co.uk/) homepage, checked on
**19 September 2026**, presents a generic “Tech, Hacks, Gadgets & Software
Talk” blog with recent password-hacking and emulator posts. It does not
identify Theo Zourzouvillys, and should not be treated as his current personal
site. The domain remains useful only as a historical identity clue because
older technical records associate Theo with `crazygreek.co.uk` email addresses.

No reliable public Facebook profile was identified in this pass. The LinkedIn
profile is the useful social-profile option; no outreach was attempted.

## Assessment

The two TermiWiki pages are very likely the same person. The strongest links
are the unusual full surname, the exact “Theo Z” abbreviation, the historical
“Crazy Greek” description, the 2002–2003 TermiSoc role, and the independently
indexed `crazygreek.co.uk` identity. **Confidence: High**, although the modern
profiles do not explicitly mention TermiSoc or University of Plymouth.
