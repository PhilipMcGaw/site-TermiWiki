# Kris Talikowski — UPSU Radio and UPSU Webcam lead

## Project-maintainer testimony

On 19 September 2026, the project maintainer identified the Facebook profile <https://www.facebook.com/talikowski> as **Kris Talikowski**, who was involved with the Students’ Union as an officer during the **UPSU Radio** and **UPSU Webcam** period.

**UPSU** is the correct name for the University of Plymouth Students’ Union. This does not resolve Kris Talikowski's formal office title, tenure, dates, or documentary evidence for the involvement.

## Public-profile corroboration

Kris Talikowski's public [LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/talikowski/) was reviewed on 19 September 2026. It visibly identifies **Kris Talikowski** and records study at the **University of Plymouth from 2003 to 2004**. This independently corroborates the profile-holder identity and places him at Plymouth immediately before the 2004–05 UPSU Radio negotiations documented in the TermiSoc material.

The LinkedIn profile does **not** visibly mention UPSU Radio or UPSU Webcam. It does, however, provide a dated Students’ Union office in the supplied experience evidence below. It cannot by itself establish responsibility for either media project.

### Supplied experience evidence

A screenshot supplied by the project maintainer on 19 September 2026 visibly records Talikowski as **Finance and Communications Officer** at the **University of Plymouth Students' Union** from **June 2004 to May 2006**.[^linkedin-screenshot] It also records NUS Services Ltd roles between June 2005 and September 2006.

This is direct evidence of a relevant UPSU officer role spanning the 2004–05 period of the documented TermiSoc-hosted UPSU Radio negotiations. It does not establish responsibility for UPSU Radio or UPSU Webcam, but makes Talikowski a high-priority institutional-context lead for those projects.

## Archive significance

The existing TermiSoc record states that UPSU Radio emerged in 2004–05 as an internet radio-station idea to be run by the Student Union and hosted by TermiSoc. It records that TermiSoc demonstrated a working station soon after negotiations began, before distancing itself from the project because of administrative and cost disputes.[^upsu-radio]

An officer connected with the UPSU side of Radio and Webcam could therefore be a valuable lead for:

- the institutional side of the UPSU Radio collaboration;
- the origin, remit, operation, and dating of UPSU Webcam;
- surviving publicity, recordings, meeting notes, or Union records; and
- corroboration of the TermiSoc account without assuming that the officer agreed with it.

## Handling and next steps

- Do not turn the Facebook profile into a public people page without a confirmed name and an independent historical bridge.
- Do not infer that the profile holder was a TermiSoc member or officer.
- Search dated UPSU material for **UPSU Radio**, **UPSU Webcam**, and the profile handle or confirmed name if one becomes available.
- If a future outreach round is specifically approved, ask about institutional records and the two media projects rather than presuming responsibility for TermiSoc's account.
