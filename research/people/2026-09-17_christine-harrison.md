# Christine Harrison

## Investigation

This record checks the identity and TermiSoc connection of Christine Harrison, who is currently represented by a stub in the curated people profiles.

## Evidence

- The [LOGin](../../source/login.md) page states that the first podcast was recorded at Rich Jeffery's flat on 24 May 2007 and that Christine Harrison "lent a hand".
- The [Back to Pong](../../source/back_to_pong.md) page credits Christopher Hunt, Chris Jenkins, and Rich Jeffery with the pilot, and says that Harrison provided research input.
- Exact-name searches combining "Christine Harrison" with "TermiSoc", Plymouth, and the Back to Pong project were checked on 2026-09-17. The results did not produce a reliable public profile that could be connected to this historical participant.
- On 19 September 2026, the project maintainer reported that the Facebook profile [chrissey.harrison](https://www.facebook.com/chrissey.harrison) visibly states that its holder is married to [Rich Jeffery](https://www.facebook.com/rich.jeffery), corroborating the relationship to Rich / `mooseblaster`. This is retained as a private relationship lead only. The profile's current relationship-date detail is deliberately not recorded because it is not needed for the archive. This is a plausible route to the historical Christine Harrison record, but does not by itself establish that the profile holder is the same Christine Harrison or substantiate a TermiSoc role.
