## Tim Huntington

The historical evidence already recorded in
[[research/sources/2006-2009_termisoc_member_pages|2006–2009 TermiSoc member
pages]] says that Tim Huntington was a current TermiSoc member in June 2008,
undertook a geology degree from 2004 onwards, and was interested in Delphi and
geoscience subjects.

The strongest public result is [Tim Huntington — Lakeside Software on
LinkedIn](https://uk.linkedin.com/in/tim-huntington-41215a34). The indexed
profile places him in Reading, records the University of Plymouth from 2004 to
2008, and describes IT engineering, application support, and database work.
Those dates and the Plymouth connection align with the historical member page;
the indexed LinkedIn result does not itself mention TermiSoc, geology, or
Delphi.

Other same-name LinkedIn profiles were found in Buenos Aires, Connecticut,
California, and the fisheries/aquaculture sector. They do not match the
available Plymouth chronology and should not be merged with this lead. No
credible public Facebook profile was identified from the search.

**Assessment:** the Lakeside Software profile is a strong candidate for the
historical Tim Huntington, based on the matching University of Plymouth dates
and name. It remains a candidate rather than a proven identity link because
the modern profile does not mention TermiSoc or the historical geology/Delphi
details. **Confidence: Medium.**
