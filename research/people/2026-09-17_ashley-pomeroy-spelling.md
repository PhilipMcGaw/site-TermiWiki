# Ashley Pomeroy / Pomery

## Investigation

The repository contains two person pages: ashley_pomeroy.md for Ashley "Arp" Pomeroy and an otherwise empty ashley_pomery.md stub.

## Evidence

- The historical account [The Abridged History](../../source/the_abridged_history.md) names **Ashley Pomeroy** as a notable joiner.
- The canonical profile identifies the same person as Ashley "Arp" Pomeroy.
- No other repository page independently refers to Ashley Pomery.
- A public search for "Ashley Pomeroy" and "Ashley Pomery" on 2026-09-17 did not identify a reliable Plymouth or TermiSoc source establishing "Pomery" as a separate person. One unrelated result does use Ashley Mark Pomeroy, but it does not establish the TermiSoc connection.

## Decision

Treat **Ashley Pomeroy** as the canonical spelling. Retain the Ashley Pomery page as a discoverable spelling-variant pointer rather than deleting it.

## Confidence

- Pomeroy is the supported spelling for the TermiSoc person: **Medium**.
- Pomery represents a separate person: **Unverified**.
