# TermiSoc person-by-person archaeology — batch 2

## Scope

Second systematic sweep of public web evidence for people associated with TermiSoc. Priority was given to explicit University of Plymouth activity/society statements and named TermiSoc roles. LinkedIn evidence is treated as later self-reported recollection, not contemporaneous documentary evidence.

## Strong identity/association evidence

### Rich Jeffery

**Confidence: High** for TermiSoc presidency in 2004; **Medium** for identity linkage beyond the historical account.

- LinuxQuestions thread dated 18 May 2004 is posted by `Rich@Termisoc` and explicitly describes the TermiSoc / University of Plymouth CS servers.
- The signature identifies the author as `Rich Jeffery`, and the signature states `Termisoc President`.
- The same thread records an operational problem affecting user creation and password changes on TermiSoc servers, giving direct evidence that the president was also actively involved in the society's technical infrastructure.
- Slideserve preserves a presentation titled *The Future of Video Gaming* attributed to `Rich Jeffery President of TermiSoc`. The current Slideserve upload date is not the original presentation date, so it corroborates the role but does not independently date the presentation.

Sources:
- https://www.linuxquestions.org/questions/linux-general-1/adduser-and-passwrd-in-fedora-1-92-is-producing-errors-182976/
- https://www.slideserve.com/quincy/the-future-of-video-gaming-powerpoint-ppt-presentation

## Interpretation

This batch materially extends the leadership chronology. Current evidence supports at least the following role records:

- Aaron Trevena — Treasurer and organiser, 1995–2000 study period.
- Rich Jeffery — President, directly evidenced in May 2004.
- Siddharth Vadgama — Chairman, within the 2009–2014 Plymouth period; exact year unresolved.
- Edward Knapp — Treasurer, 2012–2013.
- Jacob Gathercole — Treasurer/Technical Officer in second year; Chairman in final year; 2012–2017 study period.
- Elly Kensington — Treasurer in second year; 2012–2016 study period.
- Matt Farrell — Chairman September 2014–July 2017; later Treasurer.
- Jamie Woods — Chairman role during 2013–2016 study period; exact dates unresolved.
- Nick Charlton — Safety Officer 2010–2011 and Tech Officer 2011–2013.

These records should not yet be converted into a single continuous succession table because several roles overlap and the exact academic-year boundaries are not all established.

The large number of independent alumni descriptions also strengthens the conclusion that `Computing Society`, `Computing and Technology Society`, `Computer Society`, and `Technology Society` were all used as historical descriptions of TermiSoc. They do not, on their own, establish formal renaming dates.
