# Rachel Andrew — possible Theo Zourzouvillys associate

## Search and retrieval

The search began with [Theo Zourzouvillys's about page](https://zrz.io/about),
which names Rachel Andrew in the account of his early online community and
first systems job. Public searches for Rachel Andrew, Plymouth, TermiSoc,
computing society, LinkedIn, and Facebook were run on **19 September 2026**.
No private social-media content was accessed and no contact was attempted.

## Directly stated connection

Theo's page says that, while he was using Usenet and a MUD, “one of them was
Rachel Andrew”. It describes her then as a webmaster at Propertyfinder and
says she helped persuade the company's CTO to hire him as a systems
administrator. This is a modern retrospective account by Theo, not a
contemporary TermiSoc record.

The page does not state that Rachel attended the University of Plymouth, joined
TermiSoc, held an executive role, or participated in TermiSoc projects. The
current repository contains no Rachel Andrew person page or direct historical
TermiSoc reference.

## Assessment

Rachel Andrew is currently a **related-person lead through Theo's personal
account**, not an established TermiSoc name. The connection may be useful for
understanding Theo's pre-TermiSoc online and computing network, but it should
not be added to the TermiSoc membership or executive records without direct
evidence.
