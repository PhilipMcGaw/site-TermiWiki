Welcome to the [TermiSoc](termisoc.md): A WikiHistory, the site devoted to the past, present, future, previous, next, man, less, and more of The University of Plymouth Computing Society.

Essentially, this is a part-strewn battleground of things that have happened in the history of this society. it is being rebuilt from half-remembered memories… and what the internet archive managed to get.

The ¿new? WikiHistory will only really work with your help if you can add pages about past and long-forgotten events, members and servers. TermiSoc has a long (if not proud) history during which many members and servers have been and gone. This TermiWiki is an [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) Vault on [GitHub](https://github.com/philipMcGaw/site-termiwiki), Please do the pull request thing.

## TermiSoc today: CompSoc

![[Media/compsoc.jpg]]

The historical TermiSoc has closed down. The University of Plymouth **Computer Society (CompSoc)** is today’s student computing community: a new society with a closely related purpose of bringing together students interested in computing, technology, projects, workshops, events and social life.

This site is an archive of historical TermiSoc and a place to record the newer CompSoc story alongside it. Historical pages retain their original names and context; current society information, activities and committee records are collected in the CompSoc section below. The details and date of TermiSoc's closure are not yet known.

# CompSoc — today’s computing community

https://www.upsu.com/societies/compsoc/ 
## The Computer Society is a dynamic community dedicated to inspiring innovation, collaboration, and creativity among students.

CompSoc is the current University of Plymouth computing society, with its own committee and current programme of activity.

We're a fun and motivated group of students passionate about tech, creativity, and building a welcoming community for everyone.

Whether you're into coding, games, problem-solving, or just curious to learn something new, you're more than welcome — the society is open to all skill levels and all courses, not just computer science.

## Current Robotics Society resources

The University of Plymouth Robotics Society is the present-day RoboSoc. It welcomes students from any discipline, runs socials, and describes Combat Bots as its main event.

- [UPSU Robotics Society page](https://www.upsu.com/societies/robotics/) — official society information, events, membership and committee details.
- [@uoprobsoc on Instagram](https://instagram.com/uoprobsoc) — current social and event updates.
- [uoproboticssociety@gmail.com](mailto:uoproboticssociety@gmail.com) — published society contact email. Members can request the Discord and WhatsApp links through this address.

### Historical RoboSoc material

- A [2021 University project poster](https://web.socem.plymouth.ac.uk/secam/assets/posters/2021/10634586.pdf) records RoboSoc reviving Scott, an 85 cm humanoid with 20 joints and a head-mounted camera. It also notes the University’s earlier humanoid-competition history from 2004 to 2015.
- The old [robosoc.org archive capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20131210111751/http://www.robosoc.org/) is only a parked-domain page, so it does not preserve useful society content.
