# About Us

Termisoc is the University of Plymouth Computing Media Society. We have been established since circa 1994 and offer a range of facilities to our members - see our Services page for details.
What we do

We run weekly tutorials in the Babbage building, on a wide range of computing subjects. Previous tutorials have included introductions to HTML and PHP programming and to the Linux and Macintosh operating systems. Check out our calendar for upcoming events.

As well as that, we have many, many socials, from trips down the pub to bowling, to pool competitions at Rileys, as well as regular expeditions to the cinema and not-so-regular expeditions to, say, London. Again, check out the calendar for upcoming events - this term we've got a cinema trip planned every Wednesday as well as other socials planned for Thursdays and the usual trip to the Union after the tutorials. Come along - you won't regret it, and there may be pizza.

We hold the occasional special event, including LAN parties (that's 3D shooty games to non-techies); we also try and organise groups to visit trade shows and expos, such as the Linux World expo and the London Perl Workshop.

We also have links with the Devon and Cornwall LUG, and attend various events organised them (for example the ever-popular annual barbecue), and our ex-members provide a wide support base to our members. Do not ask us about the Exeter 2600, we know nothing of its existence and strongly deny any connection with it…

If you would like to join Termisoc please drop us an email at termisoc@termisoc.org, or if all the social goodness isn't enough for you check out the services that we provide to members.
