# TermiSoc on Twitter / X

TermiSoc maintained the public account **@TermiSoc**. When retrieved in September 2026, the profile described itself as “The Plymouth University Computing and Technology Society”, listed Plymouth, UK, linked to `termisoc.org`, reported joining in August 2008, and displayed 290 posts.[^profile]

This page is a curated preservation view, not an export of every post. It records the historically useful feed items reviewed so far and the twenty public photo-posts that X exposed in its Photos view. The local images are medium-resolution renditions delivered by X, not claimed to be original full-resolution files.[^media]

## Feed timeline

### 2012

- **17 November — “And so it begins.”** The earliest currently rendered photo-post carries X’s Plymouth location label. The available post does not identify the activity depicted.[^2012-11-17]

### 2014

- **19 January — exchange with Exeter Computer Soc.** TermiSoc asked `@excompsci` whether there was an award for “the most over-abundance of tech?”, alongside a photo of a table of laptops, electronics, and cabling. This is direct evidence of a friendly inter-society exchange and a technology-heavy activity, but it does not establish an event name or venue.[^2014-01-19]

  **Reply from Exeter Computer Soc (@excompsci):**

  > “if there was then you would definitely win by a mile. Full steam ahead for Plymouth.”[^2014-01-19-reply]

  The reply included its own image, preserved below as an X-delivered medium-resolution rendition. It is media posted by Exeter Computer Soc, not by TermiSoc.[^2014-01-19-reply]

  ![[Media/x_termisoc/excompsoc_424897626816118784.jpg|Image attached to Exeter Computer Soc's reply]]

### 2015

- **7 October — BCS Student Chapter talk promotion.** TermiSoc asked for support for a Monday talk by the BCS Student Chapter.[^2015-10-07-talk]
- **7 October — society merchandise.** The account advertised personalised T-shirts at about £12 and hoodies at about £21, with ordering and payment planned for 26 October.[^2015-10-07-merchandise]
- **12 October — BCS Student Chapter talk.** The account reminded followers of a 7 pm talk in BGB105.[^2015-10-12-reminder]
- **12 October — talk acknowledged.** TermiSoc thanked `@Andy0Cretan0` for giving the BCS Student Chapter talk, corroborating that it took place.[^2015-10-12-thanks]
