The TermiBlog is a rarely updated and well-hidden blog that appears on the [website](https://web.archive.org/web/20101104154258/http://www.termisoc.org/|Termisoc). Generally contains information about how tutorials and socials went, it also occasionally contains random abuse and news about upcoming events. To this date only updated by [danbjorn](TermiPeople/dan_cosser.md), it can be found by [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20101104154258/http://www.termisoc.org/blog.php), or by clicking the 'Latest News' link on the [home page](https://web.archive.org/web/20101104154258/http://www.termisoc.org/|Termisoc).

If anyone else wants to help in maintaining the TermiBlog, or has any ideas for improving the website, please email us at termisoc@termisoc.org
