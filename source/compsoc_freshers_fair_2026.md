# CompSoc Freshers' Fair 2026

![[Media/compsoc_freshers_fair_2026.jpg|CompSoc Freshers' Fair 2026]]

CompSoc invited students to meet the society at the University of Plymouth Freshers' Fair on **Thursday 24 September 2026**, from **10:00 to 16:00** in the **Rolle Marquee at the Students' Union**. The invitation welcomed anyone interested in coding, technology, AI, gaming or meeting like-minded people.
