# TermiSoc

TermiSoc was the of Plymouth's Students' Union (UPSU) Computing Media Society. It has existed since the early nineties and has traditionally had a membership of students from the School of Computing and the faculty of Technology. More recently, whilst retaining a strong contingent of computing students, students from other courses have been keen to join. Feedback from these members has indicated that they joined to support their academic activities: students have expressed to TermiSoc that their course requires some knowledge of ICT (word processing, HTML authoring and general IT skills) and have turned to TermiSoc for support in this.

We used to offer a range of facilities to our members:
- Linux Based Shell Account
- 500MB to 2GB Webspace, supporting PHP, CGI and both MySQL and PostgreSQL
- POP3, SMTP and web-based email account @termisoc.org
- Weekly Socials down the pub, at the cinema, anywhere we fancy!
- Fortnightly tutorials on a range of computing subjects
- Trips to various trade shows and expos

TermiSoc used to run its own [servers](systems.md).

TermiSoc exists to provide its members with:
- Experience of using Linux and other open-source software, as both users and administrators
- Web services, including Web space, PHP, CGI and MySQL
- Email services
- A forum for the discussion of technical and related issues
- Tutorials in technical, professional and design issues

TermiSoc has the academic backing of Brooke|Dr Phil Brooke who believes "it is appropriate that students who are interested in broadening their experience of ICT have the facilities to do so under the auspices of a student society" and therefore fully supports the activities of TermiSoc.

We also have links with the and Cornwall LUG and our ex-members to provide a wide support base to our members.

## TermiSoc and Geek Society

TermiSoc and Geek Society were separate societies until the final period of TermiSoc's history. On 22 February 2017, TermiSoc chairman Matt Farrell announced that TermiSoc was merging into Geek Soc. The announcement directed members to the Geek Soc annual general meeting and to the Students' Union arrangements for joining the society.[^1]

The available evidence therefore supports recording **Geek Society as the society into which TermiSoc merged**, rather than treating Geek Society as simply an alternative name for TermiSoc. Geek Society continued to appear in University of Plymouth society listings in 2018.[^2]

See [[geek_society]] for the subsequent history.

## TermiSoc and Robotics Society

Robotics Society, historically referred to as **RoboSoc**, was a separate society from TermiSoc. Contemporary University of Plymouth student records from 2012–2016 independently list membership of both societies.[^3][^4]

Later evidence shows the name **RoboSoc** still being used at Plymouth: a 2020 Students' Union honours listing names a student as a member of Techsoc and Robosoc, and a 2021 University publication describes RoboSoc reviving the humanoid robot Scott.[^5][^6] A 2022 Students' Union societies listing uses the form **Robotics (RoboSoc)**.[^7]

These sources provide evidence of the same RoboSoc/Robotics Society name being used across the period, but they do **not by themselves establish uninterrupted organisational continuity** between the earlier society and the current society. The continuity question should therefore remain open unless further documentary evidence is found.

See [[robotics_society]] for the evidence and current status.
