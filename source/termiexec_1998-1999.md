[Jon Still](TermiPeople/jon_still.md)
Jon is the President of TermiSoc. Sits around and moans at people till they do things. Can often be found tinkering with [Area51](area51_server.md) or keeping an eye on [Flux BBS](flux_bbs.md)…

[Rob Callaghan](TermiPeople/rob_callaghan.md)
Rob is the Secretary of TermiSoc - he keeps an eye on all the paperwork for us and jumps on people when they're naughty…a dire threat indeed.

[Keith Langmead](TermiPeople/keith_langmead.md)
Keith is the official Technical Officer and resident UNIX buff. See him in and around the [Sun Lab](Glossary/sun_lab.md), except when hes off doing his world famous Robin Hood impressions.

[Alex Charrett](TermiPeople/alex_charrett.md)
Alex is TermiSoc's Projects Officer. He's currently messing around with DNS and stuff as well as getting us involved in the compsoc.org.uk project. He's also the 4th Alex to grace TermiSoc over the years.

[Tiley Marner](TermiPeople/tiley_marner.md)
Tiley is our Public Relations Officer. He can't think of anything to say right now - so much for PR… :)

[Pete Hatton](TermiPeople/pete_hatton.md)
Pete's our Treasurer so he spends most of his day wearing red braces and shouting "BUY" and "SELL" at people in a loud voice. Possibly. The names and role descriptions on this page are transcribed from an archived UPSU TermiSoc page.[^upsu-1998-99]
