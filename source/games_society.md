# University of Plymouth Games Society

The University of Plymouth Students' Union Games Society is a current society focused on tabletop games. Its public page describes activity across trading card games, board games, wargaming, and role-playing games, with regular sessions and introductory events.[^1]

This is a current-society record. The Games Society should not be assumed to be the same organisation as historical TermiSoc or the current CompSoc without further evidence.
