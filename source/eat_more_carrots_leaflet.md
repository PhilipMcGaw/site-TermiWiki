# Eat More Carrots! leaflet

![[media/termisoc_carrot.jpg]]

*Eat More Carrots!* is an A5 TermiSoc leaflet described by its contributor as dating from circa 1994. It presents TermiSoc as the UPSU Computing Society, promotes Flux BBS, explains the society's carrot motif, and credits the fictional “Fubar Greengrocers” as sponsor.[^facebook]

The leaflet lists an executive committee using the names Case (President), Veeg (Treasurer), Nemo (Secretary), Reef (Technical Officer), and Vaughan (Letcher). The known aliases Case, Veeg, and Nemo connect with the early-history account, but the leaflet alone does not identify every name unambiguously.[^facebook]

[^facebook]: Deric Toms, “[Eat more carrots! (A5 leaflet circa 1994)](https://www.facebook.com/photo/?fbid=10159235487410192&set=gm.10154893632853785)”, TermiSoc Facebook group, 13 August 2017; accessed 17 September 2026.
