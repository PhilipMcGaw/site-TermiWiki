# Robotics Society

The **Robotics Society**, historically referred to as **RoboSoc**, is a University of Plymouth students' society concerned with robotics and practical robot projects.

## Historical relationship with TermiSoc

RoboSoc and TermiSoc were separate societies during at least the 2012–2016 period. University of Plymouth student records independently list students as members of both societies. For example, Milutin Manojlovic's university record lists RoboSoc (Robotics Society) and TermiSoc, while Elly Kensington records joining both societies and later serving as TermiSoc treasurer.[^1][^2]

This distinction is important: RoboSoc should not be treated as an alternative name for TermiSoc.

## Humanoid robotics

University of Plymouth has a longer history of student humanoid-robot work. A 2021 University publication describes RoboSoc reviving the university's humanoid robot **Scott**, which had stood for the first time in February 2020. The same publication records a history of University of Plymouth humanoid robots competing between 2004 and 2015.[^3]

## Current society

The current Students' Union page uses the name **Robotics Society**. It describes the society as open to students from all disciplines and backgrounds, with activities intended to support learning, experimentation, collaboration, and innovation. Its principal event is Combat Bots.[^4]

The University of Plymouth also currently promotes the Robotics Society as a student activity associated with its robotics provision.[^5]
