# CompSoc Best Newcomer of the Year 2026

![[Media/compsoc_best_newcomer_2026.jpg|UoP Computer Society Best Newcomer of the Year image]]
![[Media/compsoc_best_newcomer_2026_2.jpg|UoP Computer Society Best Newcomer of the Year image]]
![[Media/compsoc_best_newcomer_2026_3.jpg|UoP Computer Society Best Newcomer of the Year image]]

The UoP Computer Society announced that it had received the University of Plymouth Students' Union's **Best Newcomer of the Year** award. The society credited the recognition to the workshops, events, planning and member participation that helped establish its technology community.
