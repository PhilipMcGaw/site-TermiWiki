# TermiChat

TermiChat is the TermiSoc IRC channel. Regulars include [bma](../TermiPeople/ben_a_lee.md), [hex42](../TermiPeople/gemma_peter.md), [Skippy](../TermiPeople/philip_mcgaw.md), [Sayyan](../TermiPeople/ross_bearman.md), [chrishunt](../TermiPeople/chris_hunt.md), [spcs](../TermiPeople/stewart_starbuck.md) and [danbjorn](../TermiPeople/dan_cosser.md). Occasionally we are invaded by members of the [DCLUG](../Glossary/dclug.md), [CompSoc](https://web.archive.org/web/20101101102444/http://www.warwickcompsoc.co.uk/|Warwick's), and the [TermiBot](../termibot.md).

The channel is hosted on the [OFTC](https://web.archive.org/web/20101101102444/http://oftc.net/) network due to firewall constraints on the TermiSoc servers.

It can be found at chat.termisoc.org in the #termisoc channel. For those of you who for whatever reason can't use an IRC client, you can connect using services such as [Mibbit](https://web.archive.org/web/20101101102444/http://mibbit.com/chat/#termisoc@irc.oftc.net)
