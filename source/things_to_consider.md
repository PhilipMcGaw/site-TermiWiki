Business plan
background

The bluestamp business model is a two by two matrix, both establishing ourselfs a presence in the real world, specificly the devon and south west england region, the other a global presence on the internet and catering to both business and home users.. These arms are not independant, but we will offer the same services tailored to make the most impact on the target market. The local market is where we will begin, initially by offering web hosting services, eventually moving on to provide technical services to businesses in the area.Our business mission, should we chose to accept it, help our customers leverage the power of technology and the internet using our knowledge of computers and technology to their advantage.
