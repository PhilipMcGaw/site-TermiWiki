Termiexec 1997-1998
Jeff Powell 	Alex Charrett
Keith Langmead 	Gareth Alden
Ross Childs 	Aaron Trevena

[Jeff Powell](TermiPeople/jeff_powell.md)
Jeff is the President of Termisoc. Likes to keep busy with various projects and is normally about on [Flux BBS](flux_bbs.md) keeping an eye on things.

[Alex "Chas" Charrett](TermiPeople/alex_charrett.md)
Alex is the secretary of TermiSoc. He does all sorts of really useful stuff, helping Keith out and is currently organising fot the student union magazine to be put on the web. He is also the fourth Alex in TermiSoc's illustrious history.

[Keith Langmead](TermiPeople/keith_langmead.md)
Keith is the offical techial officer and resident UNIX buff. See him in and around the [Sun Lab](Glossary/sun_lab.md), except when hes off doing his world famous Robin Hood impressions.

[Gareth Alden](TermiPeople/gareth_alden.md)
Gareth is the Public Relations Officer, as such his duties include posters and general relating to the public (especially the female ones!)

[Ross Childs](TermiPeople/ross_childs.md)
Ross is the Projects Officer and as such is responsible for co-ordinating new and current projects that TermiSoc are involved in. This is of course when hes not off drinking his own weight in beer. And he's the Head Sysop of [Flux BBS](flux_bbs.md) - God help us all

[Aaron Trevena](TermiPeople/aaron_trevena.md)
Aaron is the treasurer and spends all our money for us (is that a good idea? - Alex?)

See who else helped run TermiSoc… The names and role descriptions on this page are transcribed from an archived UPSU TermiSoc page.[^upsu-1997-98]
