# Published Obsidian vault working rules

`source/` is the canonical, curated Obsidian knowledge base for TermiWiki. It is separate from `research/`, which contains the archaeological evidence and working investigations.

## Publication boundary

- Do not use `source/` as an unstructured scratch area.
- Research discoveries SHOULD first be recorded in `research/` and corroborated before being promoted here.
- Unresolved historical material MAY be published when it is useful to the archive, but it MUST be clearly identified as uncertain, attributed to its source, and marked `DRAFT` where appropriate.
- Do not silently convert inference or recollection into fact.
- Preserve provenance using Markdown footnotes.

## Vault rules

- `source/` is the source of truth for published historical content.
- `source/media/` is the only location for newly added media.
- `source/TermiPeople/` contains individual people profiles.
- `source/Glossary/` contains glossary material.
- Use Obsidian wikilinks for local pages and media where appropriate.
- Use standard Markdown footnotes for citations and historical provenance.
- Preserve useful legacy links and source references.
- Do not hand-edit `site/`; it is generated output.

## Historical method

Distinguish explicitly between contemporary evidence, later recollection, inference, and unresolved research. If sources disagree, retain and explain the disagreement rather than inventing a resolution.

Historical quotations and source text MUST NOT be silently modernised or Britishised.

## Style

New editorial content MUST use British English, Oxford commas, precise terminology, and the established TermiWiki house style. Use appropriate status terminology such as `designed`, `planned`, `implemented`, `simulated`, `bench-tested`, `validated`, and `production-proven` only where supported by evidence.

## Review expectation

A change to `source/` should normally be traceable to evidence in `research/` or to a clearly identified primary source. Keep the published vault readable and useful to humans; do not copy agent scratch notes or model reasoning into published pages.
