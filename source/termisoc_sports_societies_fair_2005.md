# TermiSoc Sports & Societies Fair, 2005

> [!warning] DRAFT — participant identities unresolved
> These 11 project-maintainer-supplied photographs form a contemporaneously dated sequence from a TermiSoc recruitment stand. The images establish the visible promotional material, but do not identify people, roles, or attendance figures.

The photographs record TermiSoc at the Sports & Societies Fair in the Main Hall, table 42, on 29 September 2005. The displayed material promotes “500MB of webspace, spamproof e-mail and your own web address for £5?” and the line “Call it a student discount.”[^provenance]
