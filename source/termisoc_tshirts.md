# TermiSoc T-Shirts over the years

The discussion contributors [[ross_childs|Ross Childs]], [[iain_van_gardingen|Æon van Gardingen]], and [[mattsi_jansky|Mattsi Jansky]] have existing TermiSoc people pages.
