# Plymouth Geeks on Twitter / X

The public account **@plymouthgeeks** was displayed as **Plymouth Geek Meetup** when reviewed on 26 September 2026. Its profile described the aim as “trying to create a better geek community in plymouth-join us!”, gave its location as Plymouth, UK, and showed a join date of April 2009.[^profile]

The selected posts below show Plymouth Geeks promoting local meetups and technical events and interacting with TermiSoc. They support a connection between the communities, but do not establish that they were the same organisation or had shared membership. This is a curated sample, not a complete timeline export: although X displayed 230 posts, the profile stopped loading older posts at April 2010 during retrieval.[^profile]

## Timeline

### 2010

- **12 April — community website.** The account directed followers to its Ning forums and events page.[^ning]
- **14 April — digital rights meetup.** The account promoted a 25 April gathering at Café Baguetelle about digital rights and online government.[^digital-rights]
- **16 April — logo credit.** The account thanked `@thisisthechris` for logo work. A later post asked him about the black-and-red design; its origin and any relationship to TermiSoc branding remain unresolved.[^logo-credit][^logo-question]
- **30 April — TermiSoc LAN party.** The account promoted a TermiSoc LAN party at Sherwell Hall for 15 May, direct evidence of cross-promotion.[^termisoc-lan]
- **30 November — DCLUG meeting.** The account invited `@TermiSoc` to a DCLUG meeting in Plymouth.[^dclug]

### 2011

- **26 January — TermiSoc LAN party.** The account again promoted a TermiSoc LAN party in Plymouth.[^lan-2011]
- **3 February — web unconference.** The account promoted a Plymouth web unconference planned for 5 March; the post does not establish that it took place.[^unconference]
- **14 February — games jam.** The account promoted a games jam for 24–25 February; its completion is not established by this post.[^games-jam]
- **16 May — laptop LAN party.** The account announced a laptop LAN party in a park for the coming weekend; the park and event outcome are unresolved.[^park-lan]

## Scope and uncertainty

X reported that the account had 230 posts, 58 following, and 214 followers on 26 September 2026. These counts change over time. The Photos and Videos views showed empty states despite the profile header reporting one photo or video, so no media has been included.[^profile][^photos][^videos]

The posts document public promotion and interaction. They do not verify attendance, event outcomes, founders, full membership, or a formal organisational relationship between Plymouth Geeks and TermiSoc. The separate relationship between Plymouth Geeks and the University of Plymouth Geek Society also remains unestablished.
