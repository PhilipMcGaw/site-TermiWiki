# Information For Old Members

After a rather ugly turn of events, TermiSoc is no longer able to offer membership to people who are not members of the University of Plymouth's Student Union. This means that all present Lifetime and Honorary memberships are to be cancelled at the end of December 2003 - which should give you plenty of time to make alternative arrangements for any files you currently have stored on Area51.

However, there is a way around this: UPSU offers Graduate and Associated membership. The conditions for this are as follows:

* This costs £30 per year if you are working or £2 per year if you are not
* You need to fill in an application for and return it with two passport style photographs (of yourself)
* If you are a Graduate also enclose a photocopy of you Degree Certificate or Diploma or your transcript
* If you are not a Graaduate you will need a proposer and a seconder to support your application

The Union have allowed us to provide a PDF version of the application form for you to download and complete, should you wish to. Please note that it's a form that was scanned and then PDF'd so it might look a little shady.

UPSU can be contacted at UPSU Students' Union, University of Plymouth, Drake Circus, Plymouth, PL4 8AA.

Tel: 01752 66 33 37, Fax: 01752 25 16 69.

This membership scheme is not cheap, but it will allow you to remain or become a member of Termisoc.
