So you're a Termisoc user and are totally confused. Never fear! There are a number of resources to help.

First up, search through this very Wiki. People are always adding tutorials and other help.

Next, there's the TermiList. This is termisoc mailing list, and all termisocians are members, whether you know it or not. Sign in to your termisoc mail account and send an email to termilist AT termisoc.org. All the exec regularly monitor the list, and we'll help if we can.

There's also the chat room, which you can learn about here. It's an IRC channel (#termisoc) which runs on OFTC (irc.oftc.net). There's normally at least one person in there who could help; Danbjorn and Gem hang out during the day, and Ben is generally around in the evening.

Finally, you could always corner one of us at a tutorial or social. The schedule is maintained here. Post-tutorial is always a good time to ask.

We're generally friendly, sociable people, and you can ask us anything. Please however don't be rude, and do what the exec say, otherwise you are likely to be banned from the lists and so on. On the other hand, don't be scared to ask! We'll lightly clip you round the ear if what you say is really dumb. It may even end up on the [database|quotes page](quotes.md)…
