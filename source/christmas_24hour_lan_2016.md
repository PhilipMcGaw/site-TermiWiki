# Christmas 24hour LAN & Gaming Party (2016)

The Christmas 24hour LAN & Gaming Party ran from 15:00 on 17 December 2016 to 15:00 on 18 December 2016 in the Main Hall of the Davy Building. Facebook identifies the event as being by [[TermiPeople/matt_farrell|Matt Farrell]] and the TermiSoc group.[^facebook-event]

The event description calls the organiser the “Computing & Gaming Society”, and says that Game Soc and MTG Plymouth were collaborators. It advertised computer games, Magic: The Gathering, and board games. Admission was listed as £3.50 regardless of membership status, compared with a stated usual price of £5.[^facebook-event]

Computing & Gaming Society was a re-brand of TermiSoc. A membership announcement posted to the TermiSoc Facebook group on 11 November 2016 uses the CGS name and describes its computing, gaming, workshop, social, and LAN programme. The Facebook record therefore uses the earlier group name for the host and the re-branded name in the event description. The formal basis of the re-brand still needs contemporaneous documentation.[^cgs-announcement]
