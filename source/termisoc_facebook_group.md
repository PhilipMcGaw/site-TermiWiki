# TermiSoc Facebook Group

The TermiSoc Facebook group describes itself as **The University of Plymouth Terminal Society**. It was created on 17 May 2007 and is associated with Plymouth.

At the time this record was made, the group had 261 members. Facebook identifies it as a private group: membership and posts are visible only to members, although the group itself can be found publicly. The group reported no posts, comments or reactions in the preceding month.
