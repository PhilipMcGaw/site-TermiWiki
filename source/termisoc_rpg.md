# What is it?

The TermiSoc RPG was an idea coined one day whilst Ben, Rich and Gem were dawdling about the library.

Essentially, it was going to be a tale of terror combining both the aspects of TermiSoc themselves (society stuff, pranks, winning members, etc.) combined with a much deadlier threat (giant pink bunny rabbits from space hellbent on taking over the world).
What happened to it?

It kind of all ran aground when there was a dispute about how it should be written. Rich wanted to write the first incarnation in RPG Maker or a similar pre-built engine, Gem wanted to write her own RPG engine, preferably handling 3D, and Ben just wanted to go home and have a cup of tea.

It all went downhill from there.
