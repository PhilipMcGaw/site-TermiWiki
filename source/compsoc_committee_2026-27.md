# CompSoc Committee 2026/27

The University of Plymouth Computer Society committee for the 2026/27 academic year organises the society's events, projects and community activity. Their programme includes coding and technology events, hackathons, workshops and socials, with the aim of keeping the society welcoming to anyone interested in technology.
