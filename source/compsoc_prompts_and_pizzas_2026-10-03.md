# Prompts and Pizzas: Build with AI, No Experience Necessary

CompSoc advertised a free, informal AI workshop for **Saturday 3 October 2026**, from **18:00 to 20:30** in **Rolle 002**. The event description invited students from any course, with no coding experience required.

The advertised format included creative AI prompts, collaborative groups, pizza, and prizes for creative solutions. The organiser was the [University of Plymouth Computer Society (CompSoc)](https://www.upsu.com/societies/compsoc/).
