Attending the AGM is one of your best oppertunites to have a say in how the society is run, even if you don't want to run for a position If you want to help run TermiSoc for the next year here is your chance! It looks good on your CV, but more importantly it is great fun!
When is it?

The elections are on 22nd March 2001 at 19:00 hours in Scott Building Room 002. All members are required to attend - dont worry the bar is less than 200 metres away!
What position can I run for?

There are 5 positions available on the committee, which look after the society and it's members. They are:-
President

Acts as a figurehead for the society, deals with some of the PR side and facilitates meetings with the other exec members - co-rdinates most of the society's activities. Also is the primary target in any game of Quazers, for the opposing team.
Secretary

Looks after the society's membership and any paperwork that comes our way. First point of contact for both the SU and outside organisations.
Treasurer

Responsible for the financial ins and outs. Needs to report on state of funds at meetings and also deals with the society's budget. Has to say "Guv" if you don't know how much money is in the accounts. Bank robbers need not apply..
Technical Officer

Responsible for running our 3 servers and deals with all aspects of system administration from hardware builds to software upgrades,
system security and backups. Some linux knowledge is needed for this role.
PR and Training Officer

Responsible for publicising the society and gaining new membership. Also arranges for workshops to assist members, and arranging any social events.
How do I run for a committee position?

1. Get someone to nominate you, by ask them to send a email to Adam the Secretary. (kane@termisoc.org)
2. Also get another person to second you, and also get them to send a email to the Secretary. (kane@termisoc.org)
3. Next start your campaign! You can put posts up on the boards on Flux, send emails to termilist and also stick pieces of paper up on the TermiSoc notice board in the Student Union.
4. Have fun and good luck!
Questions

If you have any questions, please send a email to termisoc@termisoc.org - we are here to help and support you!
