ILS (Information and Learning Services) was a University of Plymouth division responsible for library, information, and computing-related services.

## History and name changes

Around 2006, ILS was formed by merging **Information Services (Library)** with **Information Technology and Communications (Computing and Networks)**. The available source does not establish a more precise start date.[^ils-history]

The University's current public pages use **IT Services** and **Technology and Information Services** for central IT, while **Library and Learning Services** is used for library and learning support. This indicates that the former ILS structure or name has changed, although the exact date and administrative sequence are not yet established.[^ils-current]

## TermiSoc connection

TermiSoc's historical material refers to ILS in connection with the University's computer systems, network, firewall, and access to the server cupboard. The TermiSoc servers were reported to have been housed in a cupboard in the Babbage building; this identifies the historical location of the society's equipment, not necessarily the location of the ILS department itself.[^termisoc-infrastructure]

## Building address

The current University of Plymouth Babbage Building is listed at **24 James Street, Plymouth, PL4 6EQ**. This is the present building address; it should not automatically be treated as the historical postal address of the TermiSoc server cupboard or an ILS office.[^babbage-address]
