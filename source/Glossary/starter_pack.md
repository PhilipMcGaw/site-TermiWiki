# Starter pack

The Starter Pack was an idea by [Powell](../TermiPeople/jeff_powell.md) to give new members a guide to how to use their TermiSoc services at the same time as they were issued with [Area51](../area51_server.md) accounts.

Over the years several versions of this were produced, and although v1 (1997) seems to have been lost to the bit bit-bucket in the sky other revisions are here for your viewing pleasure.

Remember Windows 3.1 running in Open Access? We do!

Get your copy of the [Starter Pack](starter_pack.md).
