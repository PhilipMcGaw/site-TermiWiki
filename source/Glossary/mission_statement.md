# About TermiSoc

TermiSoc is the University of Plymouth’s Students’ Union ([[upsu|UPSU]]) computing society. It has existed since the early nineties and has traditionally had a membership of students from the School of Computing and the faculty of Technology. More recently, whilst retaining a strong contingent of computing students, students from other courses have been keen to join. Feedback from these members has indicated that they joined to support their academic activities: students have expressed to TermiSoc that their course requires some knowledge of ICT (word processing, HTML authoring and general IT skills) and have turned to TermiSoc for support in this.

TermiSoc currently runs three servers, as described in TermiSoc’s other documentation. In short, these servers provide TermiSoc’s members with a Web server, an email server and a projects server. TermiSoc also maintain a domain name server, which holds DNS records for the termisoc.org and the upsu.plymouth.ac.uk domains.

Mission Statement

TermiSoc exists to provide its members with:

* Experience of using Linux and other open-source software, as both users and administrators
* Web services, including Web space, PHP, CGI and MySQL
* Email services
* A forum for the discussion of technical and related issues
* Tutorials in technical, professional and design issues

## Academic Support

TermiSoc has the academic backing of Dr Phil Brooke who believes "it is appropriate that students who are interested in broadening their experience of ICT have the facilities to do so under the auspices of a student society" and therefore fully supports the activities of TermiSoc.

## Documentation

TermiSoc maintains a range of documentation. All members are required to sign a System User agreement, which prescribes acceptable use and behaviour on TermiSoc’s machines. In accordance with UPSU rules, TermiSoc also maintains a Safety Statement, a Code of Conduct and a Constitution, each describing aspects of how the society is run, how its members are expected to behave and disciplinary action that TermiSoc may take if guidelines are broken.
