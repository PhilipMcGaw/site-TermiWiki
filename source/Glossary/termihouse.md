# TermiHouse

The TermiHouse is the house in Plymouth where [Rich](../TermiPeople/rich_jeffery.md), [Chris](../TermiPeople/chris_morris.md), [Skippy](../TermiPeople/philip_mcgaw.md) and [Ben](../TermiPeople/ben_a_lee.md) used to reside between 2005-2006, and where [Gem](../TermiPeople/gemma_peter.md) and [Seth](../TermiPeople/seth_kneller.md) spend quite a lot of time. It's also the home of quite a lot of Termisoc hardware, including Banana and Marvin, (for some reason) a ridiculous number of IDE cables, not to mention [Skippy](../TermiPeople/philip_mcgaw.md)'s hardware collection, and at times the [webcam](upsu.md).

TermiHouse was replaced with [TermiHouse2](termihouse2.md)
