# DCLUG

Not only was it founded by two ex-members ([Charrett](../TermiPeople/alex.md) and [" Trevena](../TermiPeople/aaron_trevena.md)), but it was originally hosted on Termisoc servers (as lug.termisoc.org). 

![[Media/luglogo.jpg]]

[TermiSoc](../termisoc.md) provides something resembling secondary DNS to the DCLUG, and in return that nice Mr Charrett returns the favour and sends helpful emails along the lines of "you've effed it up again, haven't you?".

## The Name
Officially it's the Devon and Cornwall GNU/Linux User Group. Most people just call it the LUG, or DCLUG, or sometimes the GLUG or DCGLUG.
## The Site
Visit DCLUG's website at https://dclug.org.uk.
