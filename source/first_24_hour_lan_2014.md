# First 24 Hour LAN (2014)

![[Media/termisoc_first_24_hour_lan_2014_flyer.jpg]]

A TermiSoc flyer announced its “First 24 Hour LAN” for October 2014. It advertised tournaments including Team Fortress 2, Mario Kart, Supreme Commander and Magic: The Gathering, alongside refreshments and prizes. The flyer also describes Monday meetings in Babbage at 19:00.[^1]

The flyer is evidence of a planned event. Its exact date, venue and whether it went ahead remain to be corroborated.
