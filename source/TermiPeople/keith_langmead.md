# Keith "Penfold" Langmead

> **DRAFT — historical profile requiring further corroboration**

## TermiSoc

Keith Langmead described himself in a 2011 Register Forums post as having been the **main sysadmin for the Students' Union's computing society, TermiSoc**, while he was at university. He wrote that TermiSoc had three Linux servers of its own, located in the basement of a university building, and that several other people had root access. [^1]

He also described a security-focused member of the group who had root access and was actively working on system security. The incident he recalled resulted in the society temporarily losing remote access after permissions on users' home-directory `.` and `..` entries were changed incorrectly. [^1]

This is valuable contemporary retrospective evidence for TermiSoc's server infrastructure and for Langmead's role as a system administrator. The exact dates of his university attendance, the names of the servers, and the identities of the other administrators remain to be established.
