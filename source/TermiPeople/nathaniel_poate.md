# Nathaniel Poate

## TermiSoc-adjacent context

Nathaniel Poate, known in this context as **Nat** / **Roboteernat**, is included here as **TermiSoc-adjacent**, not as a documented TermiSoc member. His public LinkedIn profile records study at the University of Plymouth from 2007 to 2009. A project contributor identified him as a worthwhile person to retain in the wider Plymouth technical and computing-community context, while specifically noting that he was not directly in TermiSoc.[^1]

The available evidence does not currently establish a direct organisational, committee, social, or infrastructure connection to TermiSoc. This page should therefore not be used to imply membership or participation.

## Later work

The same public profile identifies later work with Aardman Animations and describes him as a mechanical engineer working in animation. This is contextual biographical information rather than evidence about TermiSoc.[^1]

## Public technical work

The `Roboteernat` site documents work on animatronics, electronics, 3D printing, CNC machining, and combat robotics. Its posts are published under the `Roboteernat` name and link to Team SC, a shop for Short Circuit robot parts.[^2] The supplied Robot Wars wiki link identifies the related **Short Circuit** entry, but that page was not independently retrievable during this update.[^3]
## Robot Wars evidence

The supplied copy of the *Robot Wars Wiki* article identifies Nathaniel Poate and Philip Johnson as the **Short Circuit** team, records the robot’s Series 6 UK appearance, and describes its earlier development under the name **NataPataBot**. The article also connects Poate’s later work with Team SC and other combat-robot projects. These details are recorded as a secondary-source lead and are not evidence of TermiSoc membership.[^4]
