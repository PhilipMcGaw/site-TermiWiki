# Adam Carter-Groves

## TermiSoc

Adam Carter-Groves's public LinkedIn profile records that he studied at the University of Plymouth from 2007 to 2012 and was involved with TermiSoc (Computing Society). The same profile records his involvement in the Rock and Live Music Society, where he was Treasurer and President.[^1]

His public portfolio identifies him as a game designer and programmer and preserves examples of work including **Salvage: The Fall**, **Einsam**, and **Slye's Lost Words**. These projects are useful surviving public evidence for his technical and game-development interests, but are not presented as TermiSoc projects.[^1][^2]

## Public portfolio

Adam's current personal site describes his career as a game designer and provides a portfolio of later professional work. It is retained here as identity corroboration rather than as evidence of a specific TermiSoc activity.[^2]
