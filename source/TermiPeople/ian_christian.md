# Ian "Pookey" Christian

> **DRAFT — historical profile requiring further corroboration**

![[Media/pookey.jpg| Ian "Pookey" Christian]]

## TermiSoc

Ian P. Christian, known as “Pookey”, described himself in a TermiSoc Facebook-group post as a former **President** and **Technical Officer**.[^1] This directly corroborates the two roles, but the visible post did not provide reliable dates.

The surviving TermiWiki executive list records Christian as President in **2002–03**.[^2] The abridged history instead describes him as taking over the TermiSoc servers in 2002, serving as President in an executive formed without an AGM, and later becoming Head Technical Officer when a new executive formed during the same year.[^3] These legacy accounts do not provide enough precision to establish a single unqualified office chronology, so both are retained.

## Preserved legacy notes

The earlier TermiWiki profile records Computer Systems and Networking as Christian’s degree subject. It also states that he worked for several ISPs after university and links a personal website; these claims remain preserved legacy material and have not been independently re-verified in this update.

## Random Fact
Ian's middle initial is 'P' but it does not stand for 'Pookey', it stands for 'Peter'!
