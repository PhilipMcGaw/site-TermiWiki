# Eleanor Milner

## TermiSoc

Eleanor “Elle” Milner was Secretary on TermiSoc's 2015 executive committee. A dated “Meet your Execs!” graphic, posted on 22 March 2015 and published in this archive with permission, uses the name **Eleanor “Elle” Milner** and identifies the Secretary role.[^1]

Contemporary 2015 TermiSoc material also records the fuller name **Eleanor Kate Milner**. The name forms are treated as the same committee member, while “Elle” is retained as the historical label used on the committee graphic.
