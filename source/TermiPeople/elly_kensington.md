# Elly Kensington

## TermiSoc

Elly Kensington's public LinkedIn profile records that she studied at the University of Plymouth from 2012 to 2016 and joined TermiSoc, the Computing Society, during her first year. She states that she became **Treasurer** in her second year. Her profile also records involvement with the Robotics Society (RoboSoc) during her first year, before concentrating on TermiSoc and her studies. [^1]
