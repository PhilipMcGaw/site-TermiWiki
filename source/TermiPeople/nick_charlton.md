# Nick Charlton

## Degree Undertaken
- 2009–2013 — BSc (Hons) Computer Science

## Positions Held
- 2011–2013 — Technical Officer
- 2010–2011 — Safety Officer
- 2009–2010 — Member

The dates above are supported independently by Nick's public LinkedIn profile, which records Safety Officer (2010–2011) and Tech Officer (2011–2013) for TermiSoc, described there as the University of Plymouth Computing Society.[^1]

## Contemporary TermiSoc evidence

Nick's personal website preserves substantial contemporary material from his period of involvement with TermiSoc.

On 8 February 2011 he published **TermiSoc Hack Weekend 2011**, describing a programme of TermiSoc sessions and projects. The article records work involving games, Arduino, open data, algorithms, natural-language processing, mobile and location services, HTML5/CSS/JavaScript, Python, Google App Engine, and Git. It also refers to the TermiSoc mailing list and says that people outside TermiSoc were welcome to attend.[^2]

On 7 March 2011 he published **The Digital Peninsula's First Web Unconference**, recording that he gave a talk about TermiSoc at the University and that attendees subsequently asked about TermiSoc and its Monday Workspace evenings. This is contemporary evidence of the society's activity and its engagement with the wider South-West web community.[^3]
