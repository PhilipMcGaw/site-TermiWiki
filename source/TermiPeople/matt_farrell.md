# Matt Farrell

Matt Farrell served as a Chairman of TermiSoc and later as Treasurer. A contemporaneous TermiSoc Facebook-group description dated 26 October 2016 lists him as Treasurer of its Computing & Gaming Society (CGS) re-brand.[^1]

The previously recorded broad Chairmanship dates need reconciliation: the same October 2016 description lists Jacob Gathercole as Chairman.
