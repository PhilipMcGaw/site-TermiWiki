# Aaron "Betty" Trevena

Aaron Trevena studied at the University of Plymouth from 1995 to 2000. His public LinkedIn profile records his involvement in **TermiSoc (UPSU Computing and Internet Society)** as **Treasurer and organiser**, and as Student Representative for the BSc (Hons) Computer Systems and Networks course. The same profile states that he co-founded the Devon and Cornwall Linux User Group (DCLUG) with his TermiSoc colleague [[alex_charrett|Alex Charrett]].[^1]
The profile also records a **16-month industrial placement with MSAS Global Logistics**, involving web integration, intranet development, and document-management systems. This is career and education context rather than TermiSoc evidence.[^1]

## TermiSoc

The surviving public profile provides independent evidence for Aaron's role as Treasurer and organiser. A 2001 London Perl Mongers archive also preserves Aaron posting under a `termisoc.org/~betty` address, providing contemporary evidence that he was using TermiSoc infrastructure after his University of Plymouth period. His posts include discussion of Perl development and an AutoDIAL tool for generating UML class diagrams.[^2]

The archive also shows Aaron describing his move back to the West Country in June 2001 while retaining the TermiSoc-hosted identity, which helps place the account historically.[^2]
