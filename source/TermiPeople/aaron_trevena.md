# Aaron "Betty" Trevena

Aaron Trevena studied at the University of Plymouth from 1995 to 2000. His public LinkedIn profile records his involvement in **TermiSoc (UPSU Computing and Internet Society)** as **Treasurer and organiser**, and as Student Representative for the BSc (Hons) Computer Systems and Networks course. The same profile states that he co-founded the Devon and Cornwall Linux User Group (DCLUG) with his TermiSoc colleague [[alex_charrett|Alex Charrett]].[^1]

## TermiSoc

The surviving public profile provides independent evidence for Aaron's role as Treasurer and organiser. It also provides a direct contemporary-person network link between Aaron and Alex Charrett, supporting the existing TermiWiki record that the two were involved in DCLUG after their TermiSoc association.[^1]
