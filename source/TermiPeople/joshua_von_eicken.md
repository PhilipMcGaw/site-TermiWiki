# Joshua von Eicken

## TermiSoc

Joshua von Eicken's public LinkedIn profile records that he studied at the University of Plymouth from 2008 to 2012 and was involved with **Termisoc (Computing Society)**, alongside the Adventure & Expo Society and Classics & Poetry Society.[^1]

The profile records a First Class Honours degree, a Certificate of Professional Industrial Experience, and the Institution of Analysts and Programmers Prize for his final-year project on software plagiarism detection using Abstract Syntax Tree analysis.[^1]

Joshua is now listed by Insly as its **Chief Product and Technology Officer (CPTO)**. This current role is identity and career corroboration only and is not presented as TermiSoc activity.[^2]
