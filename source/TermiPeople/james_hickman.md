# James "Simkin" Hickman

James “Simkin” Hickman studied Computing & Informatics at the University of Plymouth from 1991 to 1995 and identifies himself as President of the Terminal Society during that period.[^linkedin]

The RIPE NCC’s professional biography describes James Hickman (born 1972) as having moved from University of Plymouth Computing & Informatics into rail-industry IT, OmniSky, PSINet, and network consultancy. This career history is consistent with the technical environment surrounding early TermiSoc, including the society’s servers and network projects.[^ripe]

He is identified in the early TermiSoc history as **“Simkin”**, one of the original “Group of Three” associated with the society’s formation, alongside Marcus “Samwise” Adams and Paul “Fletch” Fletcher.[^history]
