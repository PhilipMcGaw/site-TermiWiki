# Jacob Gathercole

## TermiSoc

Jacob Gathercole's public LinkedIn profile records that he studied at Plymouth University from 2012 to 2017 and was involved with TermiSoc, the Plymouth University computing society. He states that he was **Treasurer and Technical Officer** in his second year and subsequently **Chairman** in his final year. [^1]
