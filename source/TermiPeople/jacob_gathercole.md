# Jacob Gathercole

> **DRAFT — historical profile requiring further corroboration**

## TermiSoc

Jacob Gathercole studied at Plymouth University from 2012 to 2017. His public university activities record states that he was **Treasurer and Technical Officer of TermiSoc**, the Plymouth University computing society, during his second year, and subsequently became **Chairman during his final year**. [^1]

This provides direct evidence for his involvement with TermiSoc and for the two committee roles. A contemporaneous TermiSoc Facebook-group description dated 26 October 2016 also lists him as Chairman of the Computing & Gaming Society (CGS), then described as “formerly TermiSoc”.[^2] The exact start and end dates of each office should still be established before being added to a formal committee succession.
