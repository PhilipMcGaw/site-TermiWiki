# Dave "Moose/damoose" Pithouse

> **DRAFT — historical profile.** Some dates and offices remain to be corroborated.

![[Media/david_pithouse.gif]]

## TermiSoc

The surviving legacy profile records Pithouse as holding **PR Officer** in 2001–2002 and describes him as holding “Everything but the President” in 2003–2004. These dates and the precise meaning of the latter description require further contemporary corroboration.

Independent surviving material provides stronger evidence for his technical involvement. A PHP/MySQL tutorial presented by **David Pithouse** identifies him as **Termisoc Secretary** and references TermiSoc-hosted infrastructure including `phpmyadmin.termisoc.org` and `termisoc.org/~moose/tutorials`. The tutorial also gives `termisoc@termisoc.org` as a TermiSoc contact and lists `www.fluxbbs.org` among sources of help. [^1]

The `~moose` path is consistent with the historical nickname recorded on this page and provides a useful infrastructure link between Pithouse and TermiSoc's web hosting environment. It should not, however, be taken by itself as evidence that Pithouse administered all of the referenced infrastructure.

## Education

David Pithouse's current public LinkedIn profile records **Plymouth University, 2000–2004**, studying Computing and Informatics, with specialisation in query optimisation and data modelling outside relational models. [^2]

## Last Sighting

Working in business intelligence; his current public profile identifies PITHOUSE CONSULTING LTD and describes experience in business intelligence and development. [^2]
