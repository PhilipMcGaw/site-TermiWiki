# Christopher Jenkins

Christopher Jenkins's public LinkedIn profile records a University of Plymouth education from 2007 to 2011 and lists **Termisoc (Computing Society)** among his activities, alongside the Musical Theatre Group and Robotics Society.[^1]

The profile describes his later work as an Android engineer, including build systems, platform engineering, and Kotlin-based web development. This is useful identity and career corroboration, but is not presented as TermiSoc activity.[^1]

> **Identity note:** the current public profile is `chrisjenx`. The older TermiWiki stub linked to a different LinkedIn URL; the `chrisjenx` profile is the one that independently matches the University of Plymouth dates and TermiSoc activity. The historical `secretary` tag is retained from the earlier TermiWiki record but has not been independently verified here.
