# Rich "MooseBlaster" Jeffery

## Positions Held
- 2008–present — Honorary Member
- 2004–2008 — [President](../president.md)
- 2003–2004 — [Safety Officer](../safety_officer.md)

## El Presidente and leader of a killer robot race.
Rich started off in this society in 2003, a lowly 0 year student, but by January he had been promoted to Safety Officer, where shockingly he didn't kill anyone (maybe due to the fact that nothing happened that COULD hurt anyone!)

In March 2004, he decided to run for President, hoping to attract people to the society—partly because nobody else wanted the job. With [[philip_mcgaw|Skippy]], [[gemma_peter|Gem]] and [[dan_cosser|Dan]], he assembled a reasonable-sized group, which was then scattered when every hard drive in every server went “AIEEEE!” They regrouped the following year.

Rich did the jobs people did not tend to notice: web work, forums and wikis, paperwork, meetings and social-event writing, while also attempting university work. He was TermiSoc's longest-serving President, leading it for four years before graduating.

He was reported to have a weird record collection, too many games consoles, a giant fake spider, unusually old goldfish, broken computers, and a Mac laptop named *Hovering Sombrero*.

He built the final incarnation of [[zaphod|Zaphod]], with later modifications by [[ben_a_lee|Ben Eskola]].
