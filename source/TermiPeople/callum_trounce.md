# Callum Trounce

## TermiSoc

Callum Trounce was **Technical Officer** on TermiSoc’s 2015–16 executive committee.[^1] His public LinkedIn profile records study at Plymouth University from 2013 to 2016 and identifies involvement in the Computing Society and Games Society.[^2]
