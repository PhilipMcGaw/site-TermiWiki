Member Notices
UPDATE 03/05/2006 03:25

[Zaphod](zaphod.md) is now installed. Wiki still requires an image directory and Horde requires TermiSoc Skin, but everything else should be up.

There may also be an issue where some e-mail has not been transferred correctly. We will retake that from [Arthur](arthur.md) in the next couple of days.

Also, [Arthur](arthur.md) has been removed for re-installation as mail/[projects](projects.md)/backup/DNS server. Will be reinstalled soon. In the meantime, [Zaphod](zaphod.md) is handling all mail.

The TermiList has been migrated to SmartLists as Mailman was clunky and starting to go awry. We will implement web frontend soon.

UPDATE 23/08/2005 23:24

Ford is now backing up to Arthur

Check out the Termisoc Wiki. It has been re-installed following Apple's demise.

UPDATE 06/08/2005 19:55

[Arthur](arthur.md) is now backing up to somewhere that isn't itself.

Been working on the events page, hopefully, will have an RSS feed up for them.

UPDATE 13/07/2005 16:09

The website layout has been rewritten in CSS.

MySQL is running. Anyone wanting a database account should email us.

In attempting to back up [Arthur](arthur.md) to another server there is a small problem. After replacing the hard drive it's storage capacity is far bigger than any of the other servers.

Please take care to backup your home directories and databases to somewhere else while backups are not yet sorted
UPDATE 07/06/2005 18:35

Arthur lives! The server itself is now all fine but is missing the home page at the moment. All users are back, but with empty home directories. Email the technical team (termisoc@termisoc.org) to get your passwords back!

Mysql is not running at the moment, we are working on that!

NEWS AS OF 01/06/2005 19:00

As of the 28/05/2005, the server is dead. So far, we know the following:

    Apple's hard drive has had a catastrophic failure, with hard drive corruption all over.
    The backups were not as successful as we'd hoped, as the backup server continued to back up the corrupted hard drive, thus giving us a corrupted backup file. Ironically, the crash occurred on the day after we found a problem with the backup server and on the day that the backup system was going to have a re-fit.
    We are trying our best to recover the lost data, but the system for doing this is far from perfect, that we may only be able to recover text, and is susceptible to loss caused by a hard-drive head burying itself in the meaty flesh of a hard drive platter.
    The temporary mail system we have in place has a minor bug in it. All local e-mails (that's e-mail sent from anywhere in the university to anywhere else) is perfectly fine. However, mail that is sent from anywhere outside the network does not get to us. Unfortunately, this is a problem caused by the university firewall and is too much hassle for anyone other than ourselves to sort out. And we can't sort it out because we are not in charge of the firewall. Grrr. The problem will be sorted out as soon as the server is running.

Hopefully, you weren't as stupid as we were, and you have a copy of your sites, etc. so it can be added back on (in case we can't recover your data). Believe us, we've probably lost bigger things than you have.

The new server, named Arthur, will be up and running sometime soon. Recovery of data will be happening over the next few days.

Oh, and NEVER BUY A MAXTOR HARD DRIVE. We've had the drive brand now for just over a year before this happened. As it had a 1-year warranty on it, it is now a 65-quid paperweight. Oh, and Maxtor takes no responsibility for getting your data back after a catastrophic hard-drive failure, despite it being a fault with all of their drives.

Huge huge big huge apologies to everybody,

Yours,
The TermiSoc technical team

Past Announcements
UPDATE 08/12/2006 15:30

There is currently a minor problem occurring where the user directories stop being writable, which in turn prevents e-mail from working properly.

We're believing it may be a hardware fault rather than a software fault, and we're trying to hunt down whether it's the hard drive or the motherboard. In the meantime, there may be occasional problems until it is fixed. Sorry.
UPDATE 26/11/2006 13:52

Zaphod picked up another nasty bug last night, meaning that a load of our web pages got infected by a nasty little iframe. We've cleaned up the pages now and are in the process of tracking down and eliminating the problem. Your e-mail was also inaccessible for a while, which is fixed. Hopefully, once we move away from Horde things like this will happen less often. If anyone experiences any further problems please notify us!

In other news, our work to kill the bug that was bringing Zaphod's web server down on a regular basis appears to have paid off: the system is much more stable now

A quick warning: while we are performing backups onto Joplin, until we get Arthur (or an alternative backup server) going *please* perform your own backups of your email, home directories and databases. If you would like advice on how to perform these backups just ask either the exec team or the termilist.
UPDATE 20/11/2006 13:58

As you may have noticed, our web server on Zaphod has caught a nasty bug and keeps going down. We apologise sincerely for this and are working hard to fix it.

After Arthur's poor performance at the LAN party last weekened, it appears that he is suffering from a major case of deadness. We'll try and get him going again soon. In the meantime, Joplin (the Sun box on top of Ben and Dan's fridge) will be handling backups.

As a heads up, we're planning to move over to a new email client over Horde. This is all planning so far, no definite date, but be sure you'll have constant access to your email in the meantime

In other news, Trillian is working fine!
