# 1 - Name
The name of the Society shall be "The Terminal Society (TermiSoc)" which shall hereafter be referred to as 'the society'.

# 2 - Objects
The object of the society is to provide a webspace and an e-mail service to the members, to provide technical help and support service. 

Also to educate them in such things as HTML and Unix.

The society may also arrange outings to places of interest to the members as well as organise social events for the members.

# 3 - Management of Funds
A. All funds must be held in an account within the Students' Union.

B. No withdrawals may be made from accounts without the consent of two or more registered signatories, including the Treasurer.

# 4 - Membership
A. Membership of the society shall be open to all members of the University of Plymouth Students' Union, where eligible, subject to the signing of a Users Agreement.

B. Membership fees shall be agreed upon each year by the society committee. These fees shall be paid into the society's Union account.

C. A member may be expelled by a majority vote of the committee of the society for conduct prejudicial to the club or to the Students' Union, or breach of the System Users Agreement of the society A member thus expelled shall have the right to appeal to a General Meeting of the society.

D. Honorary membership may be conferred on a member of the society who has made outstanding contributions to the society. It may also be conferred on any person, whether or not a member of the UPSU, who has made outstanding achievements in the field in which the society operates and whose membership would benefit the society as a whole. Honorary membership may only be conferred by a majority vote at a General Meeting of the society.

# 5 - Officers
A. The officers of the society shall be:
- [President](president.md)
- [Secretary](secretary.md)
- [Treasurer](treasurer.md)
- [Technical Officer](tech_officer.md)
- [exec#PR Officer](misc_exec.md)

B. These shall constitute the committee. The committee shall be elected annually at the Annual General Meeting held in the spring term.

C. Only the officers of the society may be signatories, one of which must be the [treasurer](treasurer.md).

D. The names and contact points of the committee must be lodged with the Sports & Societies officer and kept up-to-date.

E. The [President](president.md), [Secretary](secretary.md), [Treasurer](treasurer.md) and [Technical Officer](tech_officer.md) (these are the key officers of the society) are able to create and dissolve non-key positions as the need arises. These decisions must be agreed upon by all key officers.

# 6 - Management
A. This section shall hereafter be referred to as 'de Management'

B. The power of 'de Management' of the society is vested in the committee.

C. The committee will ensure that society policy and, where applicable UPSU policy, is carried out at all times.

D. The Annual General Meeting of the society will be held each year in the month that is known as March in the earth solar calendar (particularly that section applicable to Plymouth and the surrounding area), and must be advertised on the society's mailing list and preferably in the UPSU magazine at least 14 days in advance.

E. An EGM (Extraordinary General Meeting) may be called by the members of the committee or at the request of at least four members of the society. Notice of an EGM must be given within at least three days.

F. The committee shall meet at intervals not exceeding thirty days.

G. The quorum for general meetings shall be ten percent of the members at any point in time with a minimum of ten members.

# 7 - Alterations of the society constitution.
A. Sections 1, 2, 3, 4A, 4B, 5C, 7, 9 and 10 may only be altered with the consent of a two-thirds majority vote at a General Meeting and the consent of the Sports or Societies Association Committee.

B. Any other part of the constitution may only be altered at a General Meeting when a two-thirds majority has been carried.

C. The General Meetings outlined above must have been advertised for a minimum of seven days.

# 8 - Behaviour
The society shall ensure that any member on a recognised society activity, or acting as a representative of the society always acts within the UPSU constitution, this constitution and any laws or bye-laws relating to the place where the society activity is taking place. Should any financial payment be imposed (i.e. a fine), for disregarding the above, this will be the responsibility of the individual member of the society and not that of UPSU.

# 9 - Constitution of UPSU
This constitution is applicable only to the society and operates within the constitution of the UPSU.

# 10 - Financial Procedures
The society must abide by the relevant financial procedures of UPSU Constitution Ch.p. Sch.3.

# 11 - Safety Police Statement
The society must abide by the clubs & societies safety police statement and must produce a Code of Conduct for the society.
